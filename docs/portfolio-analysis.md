# Portfolio Analysis

Portfolio Analysis is a **local, on-demand job** that grades every gradable holding in the user's portfolio and recommends an action for each — a letter and price targets where the vehicle class is priceable, a typed role / risk read where it isn't ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)) — grounded in the holding's fundamentals, fresh web research, and the current Market Signal house view (the research stage is **stubbed as-built**, so every run to date has graded on fundamentals and the house view — [§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 3).
It runs entirely on local models (see [local-models.md](local-models.md)) and sources holdings — and the per-stock options signal — from a **connected Charles Schwab account, which is required to run the job** (manual import supplements the holdings but does not replace the connection; see [schwab-integration.md](schwab-integration.md)).

It is deliberately **prescriptive** — it issues buy/trim/hold/sell-style actions and, on priced verdicts, explicit price targets — which is the opposite of the Market Signal Report's no-buy/sell stance ([report-structure.md](report-structure.md)).
The report forms the *house view*; this job applies that view to the specific positions the user holds.

## Triggering

The job is manual, with three independent controls rather than a sequenced flow:

- **Run analysis** is **one-touch and always starts from a fresh holdings pull**: it retrieves holdings from the connected Schwab account as its first step and runs against that pull ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 2](portfolio-workflow.md#step-2-load-holdings)) — never a prior standalone pull (**a resumed run is the one exception**: it reopens its own interrupted run's pinned pull rather than re-pulling — the resume path is designed, not built; [§Failure posture](#failure-posture)).
  Every holding that enters the full per-holding loop recomputes its engine reads; cached *research*, by contrast, may be reused within the ~4-week window (designed — nothing is cached while the research stage is stubbed; [§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 3).
  With no cards selected it runs the whole book; with a **selection** active (per-card, with select-all / deselect-all — the same selection-gated pattern as Trade Opportunities' Audit) it becomes a **selective re-analysis**: the initial work-list is the selected holdings plus every holding with **no verdict to carry** (new since the last run, or otherwise carrying no prior verdict), then the safety sweep below expands that list with any required force-inclusions.
  Holdings left outside the resulting work-list carry their prior intrinsic verdict forward **vintage-stamped**, and the roll-up re-runs over the mixed-vintage verdicts at current weights ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6-per-holding-analysis-loop)) — so a handful of flagged holdings can be re-analyzed in minutes rather than committing to the hours-long full run.
  Mixed vintages are made **safe, not just visible**, by three rules.
  First, the selective run executes the **quick check over its unselected tail** (engine-only, cheap — [§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)) and **force-includes** any holding the sweep cannot safely let carry.
  It force-includes **any holding it flags** and **any holding whose sweep result is `unknown`** — a required signal family's retrieval failed, or a condition it covers couldn't be resolved, so the sweep cannot vouch for the carried verdict (the degraded-sweep rule in [§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
  It force-includes **any holding whose position side reversed** — a long↔short flip since its verdict, thesis-changing by construction, so no carried verdict survives it ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)).
  It force-includes **one that carries an unexamined evidence event** since its last full pass (the deterministic **evidence-event list**, every leg detectable by the sweep's own retrieval — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)): the ledger's *anticipated* conditions can't be the only tripwire when new evidence is precisely what nobody anticipated.
  And it force-includes **one whose capped held-name research refresh surfaces a source-backed material update** to a named qualitative ledger driver or falsifier (the refresh lane is designed, not built — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — alongside the new-holding force-include and the over-age-exit force-include below.
  One further force-include is a **one-time contract migration**: a holding whose carried verdict was authored under the retired whole-book contract (a pre-`portfolio-v9` prompt stamp) re-analyzes rather than carries — its action was a 7b-merged final that may encode retired portfolio context — and one full pass restamps the book, so the check never fires again.
  Second, every remaining unselected holding **carries its prior action as-is**: no stage re-authors a carried action (the construction-era transition rule and its context-trim carve-out retired with that stage — a fresh *add* is never synthesized from stale research, and no whole-book context exists to justify a context trim).
  Third, an **over-age** unselected holding — its last full pass older than the ~4-week window — is resolved **per action family**.
  A carried **add-family action is rule-demoted to *hold*** at the roll-up — a labeled, rule-based weaken, stamped **`action_source: rule-demoted`** so it never enters the outcome ledger as an analytical hold ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)).
  A carried **exit-family action (*trim* / *sell all*) is force-included into the run's loop** like a flagged holding: an exit can neither be safely rule-demoted — softening risk-reducing advice on stale evidence raises risk — nor stand indefinitely as current advice for an irreversible act, so re-analysis is the only honest resolution.
  Over-age *holds* stand: hold is the ladder's neutral rung, and anything actually wrong with one reaches the loop through the flag, evidence-event, degraded-sweep, and targeted-refresh force-includes.
  So a stale strong action can never stand as current advice on either side of the ladder, with its stale badge riding the card.
  Vintage ages — the over-age boundary here and the card's carried / stale stamp alike — count **whole ET session days** (the vintage instant's US/Eastern calendar day against today's, one date-diff shared by the engine rule and the card), so an evening-ET vintage ages from the session it belongs to and the badge can never disagree with the demotion rule it mirrors.
- **Quick check** is **engine-only** — no model call, no web research, no Schwab call: it re-prices the last run's book and evaluates every thesis ledger's machine-checkable conditions, raising per-card **attention flags** without rewriting anything ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
  Because it makes no model call it runs even when the daemon is configured-but-down — the same run-gate relaxation as Trade Opportunities' Quick Audit ([trade-opportunities.md §Failure posture](trade-opportunities.md#failure-posture)).
- **Pull holdings** is **view-only**: it fetches the current positions, persists them as the latest holdings snapshot, and displays them — it never triggers analysis, and it **never becomes the holdings-diff baseline** (the diff always compares the run-time pull against the prior *run's* snapshot — [§Holdings change tracking](#holdings-change-tracking) — so pulling twice between runs can't eat the what-did-the-user-do delta).
  Because it makes no model call, it gates only on the Schwab connection, not on the local-model configuration — the one Portfolio control usable before local models are set up.

Only one run happens at a time across the whole app — a single global run slot serializes the report, both local jobs, the quick check, and the pull (see [scheduling.md §Concurrent Job Protection](scheduling.md#concurrent-job-protection)).
While the analysis runs it streams into the run tracker with **per-holding progress**, the same observability seam the report uses ([run-tracking.md](run-tracking.md)).

## Asset eligibility

A Schwab portfolio holds more than equities — ETFs, mutual funds, options, fixed income, and cash.
The equity-centric pipeline (FMP fundamentals, company financials) applies cleanly only to individual stocks, and in reduced form to funds.
Each position is classified by asset type before analysis:

- **Stocks** — the full pipeline and verdict, **behind a deterministic listing-resolution guard** (built 2026-08-05): Schwab's asset type alone says *equity*, not *US-listed equity* — an account can hold foreign ordinaries and other non-US listings — while the suite's data plan is US-only ([data-sources.md §FMP — current paid-plan tier audit](data-sources.md#fmp--current-paid-plan-tier-audit)).
  So at dossier assembly ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6a](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6a-dossier-assembly)) the position's Schwab instrument identity (symbol and description — no CUSIP is mapped off the wire today) must resolve through the per-stock FMP `profile` read (the Schwab symbol looked up **verbatim** — no symbol-notation mapping exists) to an identity (issuer name, exchange) that cross-checks against Schwab's — a loop-time refinement for the same reason the fund strategy classification is one (Step 3 has only Schwab's own instrument identity); the one profile fetch also supplies the outcome episodes' entry-stamped sector identity.
  Three outcomes.
  A **resolved, matching US listing** — US-listed ADRs included, being US listings with SEC filings; the test is the **exchange allowlist**, never the profile's HQ country ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — takes the full pipeline.
  A symbol with **no FMP resolution or a non-US primary listing** (a foreign ordinary held at Schwab, or any OTC/PNK quote under the allowlist rule) re-classifies to **not-rated — unsupported listing, with that reason**: a structural can't-grade, the guard itself deterministically re-derived from the one profile call each fresh pass, never cached ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — but the outcome is *typed* not-rated rather than an evidence-floor abstention, so no research or floor budget is ever re-spent rediscovering it.
  A symbol that **resolves but whose identity conflicts** (the FMP profile disagreeing with Schwab's issuer identity — an OTC ticker collision) is the evidence floor's **conflicting-identity leg made concrete** → `insufficient-evidence` with the floor exit's full semantics, routed at the guard itself before the engine stage (a data problem, possibly transient — [§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)), so a wrong-issuer mapping can never grade the wrong company's financials.
  A guard-terminal outcome (unsupported listing or conflict) **skips the holding's remaining per-symbol retrieval** — no statement, SEC, history, or chain pull is spent on a holding the guard already routed — while a guard the run **cannot verify** (an FMP profile gap, or nothing to compare — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) proceeds with a recorded degraded input, never a terminal outcome, so an FMP outage can never mass-not-rate a book.
  Only a definitive empty FMP body reads "no resolution"; a gate, transport failure, or unreadable body is the unverified case above.
  Manual-import positions ride the same guard — they populate the same holdings model.
- **ETFs / funds** — a reduced verdict (no single-company financials; graded on strategy / **exposure**, valuation, and the house view).
  On the current FMP plan ([data-sources.md §FMP — current paid-plan tier audit](data-sources.md#fmp--current-paid-plan-tier-audit)) **`etf/holdings`, `etf/asset-exposure`, and the `funds/disclosure*` filings are off-plan**, so constituent-level look-through is lost.
  ETFs are sourced from the **on-plan** `etf/info` (expense ratio, AUM, NAV, asset class, mandate) + `etf/sector-weightings` / `etf/country-weightings` — an **exposure-tilt** read (sector / country, not single names).
  Single-name **concentration** and direct-vs-fund **overlap** degrade to that exposure level unless reconstructed from **SEC N-PORT** (keyless but heavy, ~60-day lag — an optional enhancement, never floor-bearing).
  A future **issuer-holdings adapter** — major ETF sponsors publish current constituent files fresher than N-PORT's lag, at the cost of per-issuer, messier parsing — is the practical way to buy back single-name look-through without a higher FMP tier; it is **planned, not built**, and like N-PORT would only enrich the fund path, never gate it.
  **Mutual funds degrade hardest**: FMP's `etf/info` and country-allocation contracts include mutual funds, but the sector / country weighting coverage required for the exposure-priced valuation is not guaranteed for a given fund; a mutual fund without that usable weighting set is not lettered at all — it takes the typed **`role_risk_only`** verdict on **quote / NAV + available profile / allocation data + the house view** (the classification below) unless SEC N-PORT supplies the missing exposure detail.
  `etf/asset-exposure` (reverse "which ETFs hold this name" lookup) is off-plan and dropped — it was only an optional per-direct-equity cross-check.
- **Options, fixed income, cash, and unsupported types** — marked **not rated**, with a short reason, and excluded from grading.
  Cash still feeds the roll-up's descriptive cash read, and a not-rated position simply carries no per-position grade or action.
  Weighing a material not-rated sleeve's **risk and exposure** against the rated book is whole-book work, retired from this job with the construction stage and deferred to the future portfolio planner ([§Portfolio roll-up](#portfolio-roll-up)).
  An option position **on a held underlying** is designed to link into that holding's dossier by symbol, so the equity's own verdict sees its overlay — as-built no overlay leg exists: the former construction-spine stand-in retired with that stage, so the typed overlay stays wholly designed, unbuilt ([§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 1).

A not-rated position never receives a fabricated grade; the eligibility decision is explicit and shown in the UI.

**Net-short equity positions are not rated.**
The normalized holdings snapshot carries one signed net **book-level** quantity per symbol — same-symbol rows across accounts and manual supplements net at snapshot assembly ([schwab-integration.md §What is pulled](schwab-integration.md#what-is-pulled)) — and a position held net short is a direction the prescriptive layer doesn't model: the ladder's verbs and the outcome labels all read long ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action), [§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)).
Rather than grade a short with long-side semantics, a net-short equity takes the **not-rated treatment with a short-position reason** — no grade, no ladder action, no outcome episode.
Weighing its signed exposure against the book is whole-book work, the future portfolio planner's ([§Portfolio roll-up](#portfolio-roll-up)).
A **long↔short reversal** is thesis-changing by construction — the diff never reads it as `unchanged` ([§Holdings change tracking](#holdings-change-tracking)) — and it **force-includes the holding into a selective run's work list** ([§Triggering](#triggering)), where the position re-enters as what it now is: a not-rated short after a long→short flip, a fresh full-pipeline candidate after a short→long flip — a long-side verdict is never carried across a reversal.

For a **fund** holding the engine runs a **reduced computation** in place of the equity pipeline: **expense drag** (the expense ratio as an annual return headwind), **exposure tilt** (sector / country from `etf/sector-weightings` / `etf/country-weightings`), and a **fund-level valuation**.
With constituent look-through off-plan, valuation is an **exposure-priced read** computed as an **exposure-weighted earnings yield**, `Σ(weight ÷ sector P/E)` over the per-sector aggregate P/E (`sector-pe-snapshot`, exchange-tagged).
The composite's per-sector read is a **defined blend of the exchange prints**: the NYSE and NASDAQ sector earnings yields averaged per sector, drafted, with the same convention applied to the snapshot and its history so the vs-own-history comparison is apples-to-apples — the level is a proxy, the time-series read is the signal.
It is inverted only for a P/E presentation — displayed as a **proxy composite**, never as the fund's own P/E — with the weight in loss-making or P/E-unavailable sectors **reported separately, never averaged in**, since P/E is nonlinear and a weighted-average P/E is not a sound aggregate (the harmonic form is how index-level multiples are actually built).
The composite is computed **over the covered weight only and renormalized by it** (`Σ(wᵢ ÷ PEᵢ) ÷ Σ(wᵢ)` across the sectors with a usable P/E), so uncovered weight neither silently reads as zero earnings nor lets a small priced slice extrapolate across the whole fund.
It runs under a **minimum-coverage guard** — drafted **≥ 70% of fund weight in P/E-usable sectors** ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — below which the valuation is **recorded as a gap rather than lettered off a sliver**, dropping the fund below the evidence floor's two-real-sub-scores bar into `insufficient-evidence` (genuinely deficient data, not an unpriceable class — [§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)), with the same renormalization-and-coverage convention applied to the snapshot and its history so the vs-own-history read compares like to like.
The composite is read against its own trailing history (`historical-sector-pe`): *what the fund's sector mix costs now versus what it has cost* — a **constant-current-mix read** (today's weights over the historical sector multiples; the mix is held constant because historical fund weights aren't sourced), never a constituent P/E the plan can't see ([data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)).
A closed-end fund additionally carries the **price-vs-NAV** read, applied only where the vehicle makes it meaningful — an open-end ETF's create / redeem mechanism keeps its premium small and transient in normal markets, unlike a CEF's structural discount, so the read carries signal only on the closed-end form (the CEF leg itself **designed, not built**, per the classification note below).
A mutual fund without weightings records valuation as a gap, per its degrade above.
All of it is read against the house view.
**Constituent concentration** (top-N weight, Herfindahl) is **off-plan by default** — `etf/holdings` is blocked — so it is computed only when **SEC N-PORT** is available and otherwise omitted (recorded as a degraded-input flag, never a fabricated number).
These feed a **reduced sub-score set** — **valuation** plus a **risk** score driven by exposure (and concentration where N-PORT supplies it), with **no company-quality score** (there is no single issuer to grade) — for an **equity** fund the composite can honestly price; a class it can't returns the typed **`role_risk_only`** verdict instead (the classification below).
On the priced fund path the action, conviction, and continuity machinery stay identical to the equity path (a `role_risk_only` fund keeps the continuity machinery but runs the **reduced action spine** and carries **no conviction** — [§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict), [§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)); the **evidence floor uses the fund analog** above, not the company-statements floor (see [§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)).
The **fund-form target methodology is settled** (2026-07-16, the fund slice's plan): a *priced* fund's scenario targets — and therefore its dead-money read and upside / downside — are the **v2 function over the fund's exposure-priced composite**, specified once in [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable) (the fund-form bullet).

**Priced-fund grade contract.**
A priced equity fund supplies two real sub-scores — **valuation** and **risk** — and no company-quality score.
Its letter uses the shared composite's existing missing-sub-score rule ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)): the absent quality axis is **neutral-imputed to 50**, the composite keeps the full weight sum, and the card carries the visible low-confidence marker beside the letter.
That neutral placeholder is not presented or persisted as a fund-quality assessment.
The fund evidence floor still requires both real fund sub-scores, so a missing valuation cannot leave the neutral placeholder plus risk to manufacture a grade.
This closes the priced branch without introducing fund-specific weights; the fund-form target methodology is settled in [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable).

**Funds are classified by strategy before the reduced computation runs** — a **loop-time routing decision**, made at dossier / engine time (workflow Steps 6a–6b) once `etf/info` is in hand: Step 3's asset-type eligibility needs only Schwab's own instrument identity, and the strategy class can't be read before its endpoint is retrieved.
The asset class routes the computation because one generic fund valuation cannot grade every vehicle.
**Equity funds** take the full exposure-valuation path above, *when the composite can honestly represent them* — the exposure-priced valuation applies only where the fund's exposure is substantially in the composite's market (drafted: **≥ 70% US** by `etf/country-weightings`), since an exchange-tagged US sector P/E is not an honest read on an international fund merely listed in the US.
**Bond and commodity funds** take a further-reduced path — expense drag + exposure / risk + house-view fit, with **valuation recorded as a gap** (the on-plan surface carries no duration, credit-quality, or curve data to price them honestly, and pretending sector P/E applies would fabricate a number).
**Leveraged / inverse and option-overlay vehicles** carry a deterministic **structurally-path-dependent flag** (from the profile / name — the same screen the report's movers list applies), since a buy-and-hold read of a daily-reset product is structurally unsound.
And a **closed-end fund** adds the NAV read above — **designed, not built**: as-built the strategy classification carries no CEF class, so a closed-end fund routes as a generic fund, and the computed `nav_premium` read is consumed by no score, prompt, or rule until the leg lands.
Every class the pipeline is **structurally unable to price** — a bond or commodity fund, an equity fund below the US-exposure guard (the label states the measurement, never an "ex-US" nationality claim — ruled 2026-08-13 off attempt 2's 67%-US near-miss), a leveraged / inverse vehicle, a mutual fund without weightings — returns a typed **`role_risk_only`** intrinsic verdict: **no A–F letter and no price targets** (a letter over a single computable sub-score would be a fabricated grade, and `insufficient-evidence` would misname it — the evidence isn't deficient, the vehicle class is unpriceable to this pipeline), while the **per-holding action call still applies** — deciding from the vehicle's own attributes, with the rationale limited to the vehicle's role and risk reads ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).
The classification is deterministic, shown on the card, and a class the engine can't price honestly degrades — never a fabricated number.

## Evidence floor

Eligibility decides whether a position *can* be graded; the evidence floor decides whether it *should* be — and the engine's inputs split by criticality.
**Floor-bearing inputs** — the company's **financial statements** (FMP / SEC) and a **current price** — are the irreducible basis for a grade: an eligible holding missing these, carrying **fewer than the minimum real (non-imputed) sub-scores** (the two-real-sub-scores bar — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), or with **no admissible target driver on any ladder rung** (the named **`no-admissible-driver`** gap reason — the v2 scenario-target function's driver ladder, [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — returns **`insufficient-evidence`** — an explicit abstention — rather than a low-conviction grade.
One further floor arm is **designed, not enforced as-built**: **stale prices** (staleness read from the suite's typed freshness basis — [trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable): a price is current only through the latest completed session — the FMP quote carries no as-of date to test, so the arm waits on a dated cross-check).
The **conflicting-identity arm is enforced as-built** (2026-08-05): its producer is the listing-resolution guard ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)), whose conflict outcome abstains with the floor exit's full semantics — standing ledger and prior vintage retained — routed at the guard itself before the engine stage runs.
Web-source tiers and extraction quality are **never floor inputs** — they move conviction and degraded-input flags only, with the explicit `deny` list the sole categorical web exclusion ([web-research.md §Source quality and evidence weighting](web-research.md#source-quality-and-evidence-weighting)).
A **fund** holding has no company statements, so it floors on the **fund analog**: a current **quote / NAV**, `etf/info`, the expense ratio, and — on the **exposure-priced (equity-fund) branch** — enough **sector / country weighting** coverage to read exposure — with the exposure-priced valuation under its own drafted **minimum P/E-coverage guard** (≥ 70% of fund weight in P/E-usable sectors — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)); missing those, or coverage below the guard, abstains the fund the same way.
The floor governs **evidence adequacy only**: a vehicle class the pipeline is *structurally* unable to price (a bond or commodity fund, an equity fund below the US-exposure guard, a leveraged / inverse vehicle, a weightless mutual fund) is **not** an abstention — it takes the typed **`role_risk_only`** verdict ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)), keeping `insufficient-evidence` reserved for genuinely missing or conflicting data.
Constituent **concentration** is **enriching, not floor-bearing** (it needs the off-plan `etf/holdings`, recoverable only via SEC N-PORT), so its absence lowers conviction rather than forcing abstention.
Every **other** input (`financial-scores`, peers, the revision / surprise reads, positioning, dividends, float, beta, and all analyst data) is **enriching**: its absence **lowers conviction** and is recorded as a degraded-input flag, but never on its own forces abstention — with **one named carve-out**: the consensus is the v2 target function's driver source, so a holding offering **no admissible driver on any rung** (no positive forward-EPS consensus and no computable forward revenue per share) hits the floor under `no-admissible-driver` above, the only analyst-data absence that abstains.
"Firm" means committing when the evidence supports it, not manufacturing a verdict when it doesn't — the per-holding analog of the report's Step-3 coverage floor.

**Execution semantics.**
The floor is a **deterministic engine check, evaluated once at the end of the per-holding engine stage** ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6b](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6b-deterministic-financial-analysis)) — every floor-bearing input is structured-input presence, all in hand by then, and research cannot repair a missing statement, price, or weighting — so a below-floor holding **exits the loop there**, spending no research, distillation, target-refinement, or interpretation call (workflow Steps 6c–6f are skipped).
The exit persists the typed **`insufficient-evidence`** disposition with **named gap reasons** (which floor-bearing inputs were missing, stale, or conflicting) and **checkpoints the holding as completed**, so a resumed run does not redo it (checkpoint/resume is designed, not built — [§Failure posture](#failure-posture)).
What survives the exit: the **standing thesis ledger is retained unrewritten** — only an interpretation call rewrites a ledger, and the quick check keeps evaluating it between runs ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
Any **attention flag is retained** — clearing requires the holding's next successful full pass, which an abstention is not.
The **pre-profit overlay record survives too** — engine-only state, so the exit still persists its record with the carried observation history intact: a fresh statement-leg read at the engine-stage floor exit, or an eligibility-unscorable read at the guard-routed conflicting-identity exit (the guard-terminal skip fetched no statements) ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
The holding receives **no per-holding action this run**: acting on an abstention would manufacture a prescription from the very evidence the floor found wanting, so the card shows the abstention and its reasons instead.
And **no new outcome episode is created** — there is no recommendation state to key one to, while prior episodes mature normally ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)).

## Holdings change tracking

Each run persists the **holdings snapshot it ran against** (see [§Storage and display](#storage-and-display)), so the next run diffs the current pull against the prior run's snapshot deterministically — in the application layer, before any model stage.
Both sides of the diff are the **normalized book-level rows** — one netted position per symbol across granted accounts and manual supplements ([schwab-integration.md §What is pulled](schwab-integration.md#what-is-pulled)) — so a same-symbol row in a second account can never read as a separate position or silently overwrite the first.
Every current position is classified **by quantity** as **new**, **increased**, **decreased**, or **unchanged** — by *position size*: **absolute quantity for a same-side move, the signed swing on a sign flip** — so a short reads correctly (adding to a short is an increase, buying it back a decrease) and a net long↔short reversal is never mistaken for no change even at equal magnitude (a reversal additionally force-includes the holding in a selective run — [§Triggering](#triggering)).
Cost basis is **corroborating context for a quantity move, not a second classification axis** — it is surfaced alongside an **increase** or **decrease** (paid-up vs averaged-down), while a cost-basis-only move at unchanged quantity is a non-trade accounting event (a return-of-capital distribution, a wash-sale adjustment), not something the user did, so it neither retags the position nor surfaces as a change.
That delta rides into the holding's dossier so the verdict reasons over what the user actually did — added to, trimmed, or left a position — rather than re-grading it in a vacuum.
A symbol present last run but **absent now** is an **exited** position: it earns no per-holding verdict (there is nothing left to grade), but it is surfaced in the portfolio roll-up as closed-since-last-run, so a sold-out name is acknowledged rather than silently vanishing from the run.
The diff is the app's, not the model's — the same compute-don't-guess boundary the rest of the pipeline holds.

## The position thesis ledger

Where holdings-change tracking remembers *what the user did* with a position, the **thesis ledger** remembers *why the job holds a view on it* — a compact, persisted, per-holding record carried forward run to run, the Portfolio analog of Trade Opportunities' opportunity graph ([trade-opportunities.md §Discovery memory](trade-opportunities.md#discovery-memory-the-opportunity-graph)) but **downstream and holding-specific**: it tracks an owned position rather than discovering a new one.
Without it, each run re-derives its conviction from scratch and continuity is only a value-by-value diff; with it, a run tests the *standing thesis* against fresh evidence and says precisely which part broke.

Each gradable holding carries a ledger with:

- **Thesis** — the original thesis (when the position first entered an analysis) and the current thesis, so drift is legible.
- **Key drivers** — the few variables the thesis actually depends on (e.g. data-center revenue growth, gross-margin trajectory, net retention), each tied where possible to an engine-tracked metric so the next run can read whether it moved.
- **Bear / base / bull monitor** — three compact scenarios, each with the conditions that define it, the engine's scenario price target for it, and a rough probability lean.
  The monitor carries **what must improve** to migrate toward the bull case and **what must not break** to stay in the base case — as-built these goalposts sit at **monitor level** (one statement each, beside the three scenarios), not as per-scenario fields — with each scenario's own defining conditions naming what would force a downgrade or license an upgrade, so the next run's continuity check has explicit goalposts rather than a gestalt.
  Beside the engine targets, the app also stamps **spot's authoring-time relationship to the bear–bull band** (inside / below / above) onto the ledger — the reference the quick check's outside-band flag compares against, so that flag reports a *change* in the relationship, never a standing state the authoring pass already examined ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
- **Key falsifiers** — monitorable conditions that would invalidate the thesis (the Portfolio analog of an opportunity's key falsifiers), each typed as **quantitative** (engine-checkable against the input delta — e.g. "operating margin falls below 30%") or **qualitative** (research/model-checkable — e.g. "a credible second source ships at scale").
  Each quantitative falsifier — and each quantitative action trigger below — carries the suite's shared **persistence and condition-identity contract**, stated once — contract and drafted constants — at [trade-opportunities.md §The opportunity](trade-opportunities.md#the-opportunity).
  That contract is the materiality margin and consecutive-observation count, the observation-identity-keyed evaluation state that advances only on a new distinct print or filing, the acknowledgment transition that keeps a cleared flag from re-raising off an already-examined observation, and the stable app-assigned **`condition_id`** whose state survives a rewrite when the machine-evaluable core is unchanged and is superseded — fresh streak, `supersedes` link — when it isn't.
  Portfolio applies that contract at its own seams: **the app — not the model — decides what carries when the interpretation stage rewrites the ledger** ([§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 5), and the continuity check rejects carried-state claims that don't structurally match, like any other unvalidated rewrite (Step 6).
  A **newly written or rewritten quantitative condition is additionally executability-validated** there: it must resolve to a series the engine actually computes and refreshes, under the suite's shared resolution contract ([trade-opportunities-workflow.md §Step 3c](trade-opportunities-workflow.md#step-3c-carried-forward-watchlist-re-check)); one that doesn't resolve is **downgraded to qualitative, logged, never dropped**, and carries no machine evaluation state ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6g](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6g-continuity-check-and-checkpoint)).
  The as-built **observation identities** are the series' own dated prints, never the calendar: a market-data series keys its streak to the **marks' trading day**, a filing series to the **newest reported period end**, and the expense ratio — a value that changes rarely, with no dated print of its own — to the **changed value itself**; a read with **no dated print to key** is typed **unevaluable** rather than advancing or resetting a streak, and the quick check downgrades the family's claimed clear on it ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
  Two further reads are typed **unevaluable** on the same channel, for the same reason — the engine refuses to compare where a comparison would be meaningless, and refusing moves no state at all, so it can neither fabricate a crossing nor silently clear a standing one:
  a **signed multiple** is off-scale for a threshold (a loss-maker's negative P/E is not a cheap P/E, and a negative debt/equity is liabilities past the equity base — maximal leverage, never low; compared naively the latter also read as a *clean* observation and reset the streak, first-breach date, confirmation and acknowledgment together);
  and a **statement-basis change** breaks comparability across the whole statement-derived surface — the margins, revenue growth and debt/equity plus the three multiples, whose denominators are statement lines even though their observation identity is the marks' trading day.
  A one-quarter feed gap fails the contiguity guard, the holding drops to the SEC annual basis ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), and a growing issuer's levels step with nothing having happened, against thresholds authored under the other basis.
  Each condition therefore records the basis its streak was accumulated under and, on a change, spends one pass unevaluable and re-stamps — the streak cannot carry across, because its observations were taken on the other measurement — after which the condition evaluates normally, so the gate fires once per flip rather than permanently.
  The **full pass owns this gate**: the between-run sweep evaluates filing series off its own refresh but the three multiples rescaled from the stored full-pass audit by price alone, so its values span two bases at once and it neither fires nor re-stamps.
  The annual fallback itself stands — falling back is honest, and the flip rate is a live-calibration watch; it is the crossing the step manufactures that is not.
  And each condition is **tagged by its series' cadence** (market-data vs filing), so the quick check evaluates market-data conditions on every pass and filing-cadence conditions when a fresh observation of their series has landed — a new EDGAR filing for a stock condition, a changed `etf/info` print for a fund-ledger condition, a new print of the biweekly FINRA consolidated file for a short-interest condition (the settled bucket: the file publishes on a filing-like cadence, so a condition over it advances only on a new print — a bucket that is **specified but dormant as-built**, per the quick check's retrieval note) ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
  A **third-party technology event** that threatens a holding (a competitor's or supplier's product / standard announcement) is a first-class **qualitative falsifier class** here: when one fires, it is recorded with the same sized **`technology_read`** the Trade Opportunities lens uses (substitute / complement / mix-shift, the *actually-exposed* revenue / profit pool, deployment timeline, switching costs), so "is this a real impairment or a panic-priced threat?" is tracked as a monitorable condition rather than a vague worry ([§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 3; [trade-opportunities.md §The event-impact / value-chain repricing lens](trade-opportunities.md#the-event-impact--value-chain-repricing-lens)).
  A named qualitative key driver or falsifier is also an eligible target for the capped **held-name research refresh lane** ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
  That lane binds its result to the exact stored ledger-node snapshot and may only surface evidence for a normal full pass; it never marks the condition confirmed, rewrites the ledger, or changes a verdict itself.
- **Action triggers** — explicit **add / trim / sell triggers**: the pre-committed conditions under which the standing view would change the position (e.g. "trim above the priced-in ceiling," "add on a pullback to the base-case downside with the thesis intact").
  The ledger's former pre-committed **target-weight range** and the former **`portfolio-weight` condition series** are both retired with the tunnel-vision contract (`portfolio-v9`, user decision 2026-08-14): a weight is a book fact, and every weight surface leaves the per-holding loop together.
  The series stays decodable on persisted conditions but evaluation **skips it whole** — no crossing, no unevaluable note (which would type its family `unknown` and force-include the holding every selective run), no state update — and a legacy condition dies at its next 6f rewrite, where the claim no longer resolves and downgrades to qualitative.
  The ruled direction for any future numeric tied to an action is **holding-based, never book-based** — share counts ("trim from X to Y shares"), decided from the holding alone; unbuilt, recorded here so a later slice codes against the ruling.
  The triggers are *intrinsic* conditions on the holding; whether a fired trigger becomes the chosen rung is the action call's judgment ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)), and sizing any action against the rest of the book is the future portfolio planner's.

A **fund** holding carries a ledger of the same sections but a different flavor of content, matching its reduced engine path ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)): its **drivers** are exposure tilt, expense drag, mandate / tracking fidelity, role in the portfolio, and house-view fit — not company operating metrics — and its falsifiers and triggers are written against those (e.g. "mandate drift," "expense ratio rises above the category," "the exposure this fund supplies is now better held directly").
**The ledger is additionally typed by verdict branch** ([§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict)): a **`priced`** holding — stock or fund — carries the full shape above, while a **`role_risk_only`** holding's ledger keeps the same sections with two reductions — its **monitor scenarios are condition-only** (each keeps its defining conditions and probability lean but carries no engine scenario price target, because no target exists on that branch) and its **triggers are trim / sell only** (an add trigger would pre-commit to an action outside this branch's {sell all, trim, hold} **engine set** — the engine arm's own bar, the action call's choice structurally open with departures annotated — [§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).

The ledger is **read at dossier assembly and re-evaluated and rewritten each run**, across the pipeline's deterministic and model stages ([§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline)): the **engine** (as part of its input delta) tests which **quantitative** falsifiers and triggers crossed this run — under their persistence semantics, so a crossing is a first-breach note until confirmed; the **interpretation stage** reads those crossings, judges the **qualitative** conditions from research, and rewrites the thesis, re-weights the scenarios, and re-sets the triggers; and the **continuity check** validates the rewrite.
A confirmed falsifier breach or fired trigger is a first-class continuity signal held to the same app-validation as the what-changed audit — it must resolve to a real input-delta crossing or a source-backed finding, so the ledger cannot be quietly rewritten to fit a new verdict.
The ledger is persisted with the run and survives within the job's retention like the rest of a holding's continuity (see [§Storage and display](#storage-and-display)).

## The quick check (engine-only)

The thesis ledger names monitorable conditions, but a full run is the only thing that tests them — and a full run is potentially hours long, so verdicts would otherwise age silently between runs.
The **quick check** closes that gap with Trade Opportunities' warn-don't-decide split ([trade-opportunities.md §The two jobs](trade-opportunities.md#the-two-jobs)): a cheap, engine-only pass that keeps the ledger *live* between full runs, whose outputs are deliberately **non-destructive** — the attention flag, the quiet evidence-event badge, and updated condition evaluation state; never a rewritten verdict or any model-authored content.

It runs over the **last run's holdings snapshot and ledgers** — no Schwab pull, since it tests theses, not the book — and retrieves **by asset type**.
It retrieves prices for every holding (the job-time FMP `quote` plus the FMP **dated-EOD** closes — the sweep never reads the shared price cache, so it spends two FMP calls per holding) and the run-level **`DGS2` and `DGS10` prints** (one FRED call each on the **shared FRED credential** — [configuration.md](configuration.md)).
FRED presence is a precondition of the local-suite gate ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 1](portfolio-workflow.md#step-1-job-start-and-gate)), so it is always configured where Portfolio runs at all, and a failed call **fail-softs to the freshest cached print** — a prior quick check's own successful print first, else the last run's — with its as-of date, eligible only within the drafted **rate-cache max age** ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)); none eligible reads the rate-dependent families `unknown`.
Per **stock** it retrieves the SEC EDGAR filing check (CIK-gated — [data-sources.md §SEC EDGAR](data-sources.md#sec-edgar)), the lightweight **revision preflight** (an `analyst-estimates` snapshot), an **`earnings` re-pull**, and a **statement-and-`dividends` re-pull where the EDGAR check surfaced a new filing** (the income and balance-sheet series plus the dividend record).
As-built there is **no cash-flow re-pull**; the hurdle's payout term is the **dividend** leg alone (buybacks fold into the per-share drivers, not a separate payout leg — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), and it refreshes between full passes on filing cadence.
So a filing-cadence condition's fresh observation arrives with the *value* the condition reads, not just the fact of the filing, and the dividend leg refreshes on filing cadence, where it mostly moves: declarations typically ride quarterly earnings, and a mid-quarter change *often* files as an 8-K — often, not always, since the 8-K item is optional, so a payout change with no accompanying filing waits for the next full pass rather than being guaranteed-fresh here.
Per stock it also retrieves a **`news/stock` pull for holdings carrying a standing technology-class falsifier** (the qualifying-news-seed leg), and a **conditional FINRA short-interest refresh** — the keyless once-per-run consolidated file ([data-sources.md §FINRA](data-sources.md#finra-short-interest)), fetched only when some holding carries a validated short-interest-fed quantitative condition, each such holding then reading it as a local lookup; a failed file fetch reads those conditions `unknown`, the standard degraded-sweep state.
**As-built this FINRA leg is dormant and unbuilt**: the closed engine series surface carries no short-interest series ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)), so no quantitative condition can validate as short-interest-fed at Step 6g and the trigger never arms; the leg activates only when a short-interest series joins the engine surface.
These are the calls that make the evidence-event list's legs deterministically detectable — the estimate and earnings series are company-scoped, so those legs are equity legs ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
Per **fund** it retrieves a light **fund refresh** — **unconditionally: `etf/info` plus both weighting sets for every fund** (the equity-branch and condition gating live on the **evaluation side**, each comparison independently gated on what it actually derives from; the two extra calls per non-equity fund per sweep are the accepted cost of data-in-hand robustness).
The weightings matter per **equity** fund on either verdict branch — the US-guard crossing flips the branch in **both** directions, since a priced fund can fall below it and a role-only international equity fund can rise above it, and a material sector shift is an evidence event wherever exposure is load-bearing — and, for a non-equity fund, where a ledger condition reads exposure, so fund-ledger conditions like an expense-ratio rise, and the fund evidence-event legs, are evaluable.
Each holding's "since the last full pass" boundary is its effective vintage's **ET session date** — the calendar day the pass belongs to in market (US/Eastern) time, never the UTC date an evening-ET pass has already rolled into — and the boundary day itself is **inclusive** across the filing / earnings / news legs: date-only feeds cannot place a same-day item before or after the pass, so an item that landed after the pass ran is never permanently hidden.
The accepted cost is the mirror case: a boundary-day item the pass already saw re-raises its badge on every later sweep — and force-includes the holding on a selective run — until the holding's next full pass on a **later** ET day advances the vintage past the boundary (a same-day re-pass re-lands on it), so the noise bound is one redundant forced re-analysis plus recurring badges until that pass, not a calendar day.

It then evaluates deterministically, per holding:

- every **quantitative falsifier and action trigger** in the ledger — all engine-resolvable by construction: only a condition that passed Step 6g's executability validation carries the quantitative type ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)), so the promise is total over conditions the sweep can actually evaluate — under the persistence semantics and at each condition's cadence — market-data conditions on every pass, filing-cadence conditions when a fresh observation of their series lands (a stock's new EDGAR filing; a fund's changed `etf/info` print) — a **confirmed breach** or a **fired trigger** flags the holding, while a lone noisy print logs a quiet first-breach note;
- the **capital-efficiency / dead-money read** — **priced verdicts only** (the `role_risk_only` branch carries no hurdle read) — re-derived on a **stated basis**: the fresh price and `DGS2` print, with the v2 scenario multiples **re-anchored closed-form on the fresh `DGS10`** against the **stored** anchor-window percentiles and **stored** drivers from the last full pass (no re-estimation — one extra FRED print, no new heavy retrieval), the dividend leg still refreshed only when a new filing lands (the statement-and-dividends re-pull above — the cadence it actually moves on), the stored drivers held whole until the next full pass (buybacks fold into the per-share drivers — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable) — so there is no separate buyback leg to refresh), so the read never mixes a fresh short-rate hurdle with a stale long-rate multiple; a holding newly crossing into the hurdle's **fails** state flags (an indeterminate read never flags).
  The dividend refresh reads the adapter's typed distinction: a **`None` dividend record with no recorded gap is the adapter's confirmed non-payer** — the leg re-reads zero, so a dividend elimination reaches the hurdle — while a failed pull (a recorded gap) **keeps the stored leg** rather than fabricating an elimination out of an outage;
- the **scenario bands** — likewise **priced only** (a `role_risk_only` ledger carries no band) — the flag fires on a **change in spot's relationship to the band** (inside / below / above) since the ledger was authored, compared against the **authoring-time relation the app stamped beside the engine targets** ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)): a price that leaves the band, re-enters it, or crosses to its other side flags (the scenario read is stale in a way worth a fresh look), while a band **authored with spot already outside it does not re-flag while that relationship stands** — the model wrote the ledger seeing it, so the standing state is an examined observation, and re-raising it every sweep (force-including the holding on every selective run until re-analysis authored the same band again) would be noise, not signal; a pre-stamp ledger reads as authored-inside, preserving the prior behavior.
  The tested band is the **last full pass's stored monitor band, deliberately frozen** — the fresh-`DGS10` re-anchor above serves the hurdle read only and never rewrites the ledger's authored scenarios.

A flagged holding surfaces an amber, actionable **attention flag** on its card — the Portfolio analog of Trade Opportunities' *Consider Deep Audit* warning — suggesting a selective re-analysis.
The flag (and which trigger raised it) persists with the holding until the next **successful full pass over it** (a full run, or a selective run that includes it — [storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage)); that pass consumes the triggering observation in interpretation / continuity, records it in the verdict and ledger audit, clears the flag on completion, and stamps the **acknowledging observation** into each confirmed condition's evaluation state.
A full pass therefore **never creates a fresh persisted attention flag from the same engine evaluation it just interpreted**: the condition is already part of that pass's authored result.
The acknowledged breach cannot re-raise on the next quick check — it re-raises only on a breach confirmed against a *later* observation (the shared contract's acknowledgment transition — [§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)).
Separately from the amber flag, the quick check surfaces an **unexamined evidence event** its sweeps see, with no full pass since ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable) defines the list), as a **quiet, informational badge** in the *Research stale* family, never the amber action color: every holding reports quarterly, so folding this into the attention flag would blanket the book each earnings season and dilute the flag reserved for actual problems.
The quick check **never rewrites a verdict, grade, or any model-authored ledger content** — with one deliberate carve-out: it **updates each condition's evaluation state** (the observation-identity-keyed breach streak, first-breach and confirmation timestamps — [§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)), which is engine state, not authored content, exactly as Trade Opportunities' cheap re-derivation freezes the authored fields while running the evaluations.
Streaks advance **only on a distinct new observation** (a new trading day's print, a new filing), so repeated quick checks against the same quote or filing can never confirm a breach.

**A failed retrieval is typed, never a silent clear.**
Each holding's sweep concludes in one of three states per required signal family — **`fresh_clear`** (the family was **successfully checked** and nothing fired: a new observation evaluated clean, **or a successful retrieval confirming no unseen observation exists** — the routine same-session re-check, weekend, or no-new-filing case, which vouches for the carried verdict precisely because conditions advance only on a distinct new observation, so nothing could have fired), **`flagged`**, or **`unknown`** (the family's retrieval **failed** and the cache held no newer observation, so the sweep could not vouch either way: a fail-soft to already-evaluated cached data produces no new observation, which is a non-detection, not a clear).
Within a family, `fresh_clear` is claimed only when **every condition the family covers actually evaluated**: an allowed-but-unresolvable condition — typically one with **no dated print to key its observation identity** ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)) — is typed **`unevaluable_series`** and **downgrades the family's claimed clear**, so a sweep never vouches through a condition it silently skipped.
The `unknown` state is load-bearing for selective runs: the holding is **force-included into the work list exactly like a flagged one** ([§Triggering](#triggering)) — the sweep's job there is to vouch for the carried verdicts, and a verdict it couldn't check must never stand on the sweep's silence — and the card carries a quiet **degraded-sweep note** naming the families that couldn't be checked (the *Research stale* badge family, never the amber action color — [interface.md §Connection status](interface.md#connection-status-local-suite)).
For a stock, the filing family additionally reads `unknown` when the symbol's **CIK is unresolved** ([data-sources.md §SEC EDGAR](data-sources.md#sec-edgar)), so a missing filing feed surfaces as unverifiable rather than reading as no-new-filings.
It holds the single global run slot and runs in the run tracker like any job; because it makes **no model call** it skips the daemon-connectivity check and runs configured-but-down, and because it does **no web research** it triggers no pre-run SearXNG notice ([interface.md §Connection status](interface.md#connection-status-local-suite)).
The **qualitative** falsifiers stay a full pass's job — they are research-checkable only; the held-name refresh lane may surface a possible update and force that pass, but never evaluates or confirms the condition itself.

## The per-holding pipeline

This section covers the per-holding pipeline's **design**; the full job's **control flow** — every step, its Type (Computed / API retrieval / Local-model call), and each local-model-call contract — is the canonical spec in [portfolio-workflow.md](portfolio-workflow.md) (the per-holding loop is its Step 6; the six numbered stages below map to workflow sub-steps **6a–6g**, with stage 5 here spanning the workflow's separate target-refinement [6e] and interpretation [6f] steps).

Each holding is processed through a chain of distilled, schema-validated hand-offs (see [local-models.md §Context-memory discipline](local-models.md#context-memory-discipline)):

1. **Dossier assembly (application layer — deterministic, with a non-reasoning embedding lookup for continuity retrieval).**
   The app — not the model — assembles the holding's evidence packet, holding the pure-stage boundary; the only model touch is the fixed embedder vectorizing the continuity query, no reasoning (see [portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6a](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6a-dossier-assembly)).
   The packet carries the position and its prior-run **position delta** ([§Holdings change tracking](#holdings-change-tracking)).
   It carries any **same-underlying option positions** from the same Schwab pull, linked deterministically by underlying symbol and carried as a **typed overlay** — `{contract, direction, quantity, strike, expiry, delta, coverage ratio}`, classified **covered-call / protective-put / collar / other** (a naked short call must never read as covered; an unrecognized multi-leg reads `other` with its net delta).
   The overlay changes what the right action is, so the verdict and the action call are designed to see it.
   It carries the per-symbol **fundamentals / revision / positioning / price** surface (equity holdings draw the full FMP + SEC surface, funds the reduced ETF surface — [§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility); every endpoint, by source and cardinality, is in [data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface) and [portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6a](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6a-dossier-assembly)), with **SEC EDGAR** the authoritative cross-check behind FMP's working feed (as-built a **fill-only merge** — SEC values fill absent FMP lines; a conflicting-value cross-check is designed, not built).
   It carries the Market Signal **house view**, loaded *deterministically* (the report's own sections + summaries, never vector-searched — [local-models.md §Context-memory discipline](local-models.md#context-memory-discipline)) and **freshness-gated** (dropped past one week — [portfolio-workflow.md §Step 5](portfolio-workflow.md#step-5-load-shared-context)).
   And it carries the **prior run's verdict and thesis ledger** ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)).
   As-built the dossier's overlay leg is not assembled and **no stand-in exists**: the former construction-spine classifier (covered-call / protective-put / collar with coverage ratio, no per-leg delta) retired with that stage (`portfolio-v9`), so the typed overlay reaches nothing until the dossier leg is built.
   Three market inputs — the **risk-free rate**, **commodity prices**, and **CFTC positioning** — are designed to be fetched **once per run and shared across every holding** rather than re-requested per symbol, the load-relief that keeps the per-holding budget small; as-built the **rate anchors alone** are fetched — the commodity and positioning loads land with their consumers ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 5](portfolio-workflow.md#step-5-load-shared-context)).
2. **Deterministic financial analysis (application layer).**
   The shared Rust engine (also Trade Opportunities' — [trade-opportunities.md](trade-opportunities.md)) computes every number in three layers, the load-bearing decision being *which layer touches the grade*.
   **(a) The grade core** — for a stock, fundamentals and valuation multiples → the real **quality / valuation / risk** sub-scores; for a priced equity fund, the reduced engine → real **valuation / risk** plus the neutral-imputed absent quality axis under the [fund-grade contract](#asset-eligibility) — and scenario **price targets** (the rate-anchored v2 scenario-target function, `DGS10`-anchored — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
   **(b) A conviction layer** — revision velocity, rating drift, earnings surprise, the engine's **forensic flags**, and the **momentum / market-setup read** (price trend and setup, computed alongside the grade core but held **out of the letter** — context that can temper or corroborate conviction, never raise it).
   Those feed the **narrative-vs-reality ratio** (revision pace vs multiple — *justified-expensive* vs *hype*; falling back to the **operating-reality-vs-price** form where analyst coverage is too thin to read revisions — [trade-opportunities.md §The two non-negotiables](trade-opportunities.md#the-two-non-negotiables)) and the **implied-expectations read** — the growth / margin trajectory the current price *already assumes*, solved by inverting the engine's scenario math at the live price and expressed as a **range under stated assumptions**, never one number (the shared primitive Trade Opportunities computes at Step 5c), the priced-in anchor the trim-a-winner judgment leans on.
   **(c) Positioning context** — insider / congressional / FINRA short interest / the Schwab options signal.
   Layers (b) and (c) are **computed but held *out* of the sub-scores** (positioning until the outcome-learning scorecard proves it adds value — [§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)), so new signals sharpen *conviction and risk* without moving the letter grade — the three-layer engine's whole point.
   As-built, layer (b)'s computed reads are the **momentum / market-setup read** and the **pre-profit overlay**, and layer (c)'s is the **Schwab options signal**; the revision / rating / surprise series, the forensic flags, the narrative-vs-reality and implied-expectations reads, and the insider / congressional / FINRA feeds are designed, landing with their data legs ([data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)).
   For a stock meeting the deterministic eligibility rule, layer (b) also carries a **pre-profit execution / financing overlay**: statement-derived cash runway, gross-margin progression, capital intensity, and split-adjusted dilution first, then any app-validated production / delivery / bookings / backlog / reservation / guidance observations research adds at Step 5.
   The overlay is conviction / risk / action context only — never another grade component and never a license for the model to calculate a number.
   From the targets the engine derives the **capital-efficiency / dead-money read** — the base-case forward **total return** (the v2 function's `TR_base` directly — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) against a DGS2-anchored, risk-tier-scaled hurdle, returning the three-state **clears / indeterminate / fails** verdict (only *fails* is dead money — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — also kept out of the grade and fed to the action call's evidence digest ([§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)); the targets and this read are **provisional**, recomputed if research refines the base case (Step 5), while the backward-looking sub-scores are final.
   The deterministic **input delta** the continuity audit (Step 6) holds every verdict move to is, as-built, the position delta, the thesis ledger's **quantitative** falsifier / trigger crossings evaluated under their persistence semantics, and the prior run's stored values rendered beside this run's for the interpretation call to compare; the engine-computed metric-level diff and the **technology-event pre-flag** (fired when the holding's sector-relative move exceeds the volatility-scaled threshold) are **designed, not built**, the pre-flag landing with its benchmark context loads ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger), [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
   The dossier's **absolute street opinions** (consensus target *level*, buy / hold / sell rating, FMP's ratings snapshot) are deliberately **not** engine inputs — they ride into interpretation (Step 5) as *evidence the model weighs against the engine's own read*, never numbers to adopt (designed — the target-consensus / ratings rows are not yet pulled, so as-built the only consensus the prompt carries is the target function's forward-estimates read; [data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)).
   These computed values are the source of every **engine-arm** number in the verdict; since `portfolio-v7` the model arm authors its own sub-scores and target bands beside them, typed model-authored and never altering or binding the engine baseline (the boundary statement in [§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict); [local-models.md §Context-memory discipline](local-models.md#context-memory-discipline)).
3. **Bounded web research** — **stubbed as-built** (a single research-deferred note; the loop below is the research slice's binding design) — the 122B reasoner (thinking mode) over the bounded per-topic research loop ([web-research.md](web-research.md)), fail-soft, grounded by the deterministic SEC/FMP financials so research *fills the gaps the numbers leave* rather than substituting a story.
   Its agenda: competitive / business position, recent results and estimate revisions, catalysts and risks; **management quality and capital allocation** (guidance *delivered* vs *promised*, candor in bad quarters, buyback / dividend behavior — the held-name form of Trade Opportunities' investor-judgment lens, [trade-opportunities.md §The lenses](trade-opportunities.md#the-lenses)); the **market narrative and sentiment** as a *sourced qualitative* read (how much of the price reflects emotion about what *might* come versus present fundamentals — distinct from the quantitative options / momentum proxies); and **forward opportunity and thematic fit** (TAM, optionality, theme exposure).
   A stock on the deterministic **pre-profit execution / financing overlay** additionally receives one focused topic over the operating proof its business actually reports — production / deliveries where applicable, bookings / backlog / reservations, guidance attainment, unit economics, gross-margin progress, cash burn / runway, capital spending, and dilution — so an execution break or financing dependency can reach the verdict before a trailing annual score fully reflects it ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
   A **fund** holding's agenda swaps these company-centric topics for fund-flavored ones matched to its ledger's driver set ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)): mandate / strategy and manager changes, expense and structure versus its category, how well the exposure it supplies fits the house view — and whether it would be better held directly — and, for a closed-end fund, the discount and distribution coverage; the conditional technology-event topic below is an **equity topic** (its pre-flag and `technology_read` size a single company's exposure, not a wrapper's).
   **Conditionally**, when the holding moved on a third-party **technology event** (a competitor's or supplier's announcement that repriced the name, the same materiality gate Trade Opportunities applies), the agenda adds a **technology-event impact assessment** — reading the actual technology and *sizing the holding's real exposure* into a typed **`technology_read`** ([trade-opportunities.md §The event-impact / value-chain repricing lens](trade-opportunities.md#the-event-impact--value-chain-repricing-lens)) — which is what separates a **panic drop from a genuine impairment** (and, symmetrically, overstated-benefit euphoria on the upside); it feeds the conviction read and a thesis-ledger falsifier ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)), dormant otherwise.
   **The trigger is defined, not assumed** — Portfolio runs none of the discovery news routes that fire Trade Opportunities' materiality gate, so the conditional topic is added by any of: the engine's deterministic **event pre-flag** in the input delta (a sector-relative move exceeding the volatility-scaled threshold — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)); a **technology-class falsifier already standing in the ledger**; the symbol-scoped structured **news-feed seed** surfacing a qualifying announcement ([data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface); a seed is a lead, never evidence — [web-research.md §The research loop and context management](web-research.md#the-research-loop-and-context-management)); or **mid-loop escalation**, where another topic's findings surface the event and the model proposes the follow-up, which the orchestrator approves like any follow-up pass.
   Research effort is also **graduated, not uniform**: a holding qualifies for **research reuse** — the loop and distillation skip, its cached distilled findings and still-credited forward assumption carried into interpretation — when its research is younger than the **~4-week freshness window** *and* nothing moved (position unchanged, no attention-trigger condition, no event pre-flag, no new evidence event, and no material update from the pre-loop held-name refresh — any leg of the [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable) evidence-event list or a validated refresh result invalidates the cache); anything moving, or research aging past the window (the built-in max-age), earns the full agenda, and every reuse decision is logged in the audit record ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
4. **Distillation** — the reasoner in **non-thinking mode** consolidates each topic's **complete** findings into the compact object interpretation reads (never a re-distillation of in-loop summaries), single-pass or hierarchical by the deterministic shared rule ([web-research.md §The research loop and context management](web-research.md#the-research-loop-and-context-management)); Portfolio has no cross-lens contradiction check, so the reduce here is pure consolidation.
   Running it on the same resident 122B avoids per-holding model swaps — the 35B fast tier is a benchmark-gated option ([local-models.md §The model roster and per-task routing](local-models.md#the-model-roster-and-per-task-routing)).
   For an overlay-eligible stock, distillation may also emit typed, sourced **`pre_profit_execution_observation`** rows for the non-standardized operating facts the engine cannot retrieve itself; the app validates issuer, metric identity, period, units, source, and comparison basis before any row enters the holding's observation history.
5. **Interpretation and grading.**
   First a **deterministic target and overlay refinement**: if distillation surfaced a typed **`research_forward_assumption`** (a sourced forward fact the structured feeds lacked), the **engine — not the model —** recomputes the affected scenario target with it as an explicit, logged assumption and refreshes the capital-efficiency / dead-money read; for an overlay-eligible stock it also validates and merges the typed operating observations, then recomputes the execution / financing states and their rule consequences ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6e](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6e-deterministic-target-refinement)).
   The backward-looking sub-scores are untouched, and a `role_risk_only` holding skips both priced-stock refinements.
   Then the 122B in thinking mode interprets the refined analysis and the distilled research into the **intrinsic verdict** — grade, conviction, horizon, and the justified base-case target (for a `role_risk_only` holding the same call authors the union's other branch — the role / risk assessment and its rewritten fund ledger, [§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict)) — the engine's numbers riding as the incorruptible baseline arm while the model authors its own arm beside them (sub-scores, derived letter, target bands — [§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)); the dedicated **action call** then decides the holding's rung from the finished verdict plus the profile ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).
   This stage also **rewrites the thesis ledger** — thesis, bear/base/bull monitor, falsifiers, and triggers — in light of what the run learned.
6. **Continuity check.**
   An **app-layer validator**, not just a recorder: every verdict move interpretation labels **external** (market data, company information, or research / narrative) must resolve to a concrete **input-delta** entry, a source-backed research finding, or the logged forward assumption — otherwise it is **downgraded to a labeled self-correction** (or fails schema validation), so a no-new-facts swing can't be laundered as "the market changed."
   Output stays firm run-to-run, the self-correction the earned, flagged exception ([thesis-continuity.md](thesis-continuity.md)).
   The same rule **validates the thesis-ledger rewrite** — every falsifier marked tripped and every trigger marked fired must map to the engine's deterministic crossing (quantitative) or a source-backed finding (qualitative), so the ledger can't be quietly rewritten to fit the verdict ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)).

## The holding verdict

Each holding's output is a structured, schema-validated record that deliberately keeps two things apart — the **intrinsic verdict** (what the holding is worth on its own merits, investor-independent) and the **portfolio action** (what to do about it, decided from that verdict plus the investor profile — still one holding at a time).
Since `portfolio-v9` the action is deliberately **tunnel-vision** (user decision 2026-08-14): the job never weighs one holding against another, and the whole-book reconciliation — "trim the A-grade because it is oversized, crowded, or duplicative" — belongs to the future **portfolio-planner** job, which will read this job's report beside the market report and Trade Opportunities.
Separating verdict from action — and auditing each on its own — is what keeps an "A-grade" from silently becoming "add aggressively."

Inside a **`priced`** intrinsic verdict, two reads are likewise kept **orthogonal** — the **grade** (a *backward / current* quality anchor, computed deterministically from the trailing financials and valuation) and the **forward outlook** (a *forward* scenario read — the horizon outlooks and scenario price targets, refined by research).
They answer different questions: *what the business is* versus *what the market is likely to pay for it next*.
A low grade with a strong forward outlook is a **legitimate, first-class state, not a contradiction** — the market routinely prices the future, so a C- or D-graded business mid-inflection can carry a constructive outlook; the card shows the two side by side and never collapses the forward read into the letter (or the letter into the forward read).
This is the per-holding form of the suite's **four-part model** — **grade** (backward anchor) · **forward outlook** (scenario, research-informed) · **conviction** (the evidence-weighted synthesis, which moves *both* up and down) · **action** (the portfolio-aware decision over all three).

**The two-arm contract (`portfolio-v7`).**
Since 2026-08-05 the priced verdict carries every judgment field **twice**, as two arms with distinct provenance — the successor to the retired "the model interprets, never invents numbers" invariant:

- The **engine arm** is the deterministic baseline — a disclosed calculator over primary-source data: the sub-scores and composite letter, the scenario targets, and mechanical stand-ins for the three fields only the model used to author — a trailing-return **outlook** (~21/126/252-session windows with per-window flat thresholds), a degradation-count **conviction** (data completeness as confidence: 0 flags → High, 1–2 → Medium, ≥3 → Low), and a rung-rule **action** (grade × hurdle × admission, tiebreak toward hold; the engine arm always obeys its own bars).
  All stand-in constants are calibratable ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
- The **model arm** is the model's own read of the same fields, authored with the engine's values in the prompt as **evidence, never bounds**, and **structurally validated only** (types and enums — no value bound, band, ceiling, or clamp applies): its own four sub-scores on the shared 0–100 scale, its letter derived app-side from its own quality/valuation/risk through the shared cutoffs (comparability by construction), its own one-/twelve-month base/bear/bull target bands persisted exactly as returned (an inverted pair renders annotated; scoring reads it as `(min, max)`), its conviction, the three horizon reads, the action call's rung on the full ladder, and a retrospective `self_assessment`.

Three rules keep the experiment honest.
**Model-arm judgment values never alter or bind the engine baseline** — the quick check, hurdle tests, monitor-band stamps, and outcome labels read engine values only, and ledger evaluation compares engine observations against app-validated thresholds, so the baseline stays an incorruptible control arm.
Deterministic machinery downstream of the model's **choices** is intentional, not an exception to that line: the episode lifecycle keys on the authored action, the model letter derives from the model's sub-scores through the shared cutoffs, and the scoreboard scores both arms.
Distinct from the unrestricted arm, the **typed, app-validated channels** deliberately carry model-produced values *into* the engine — the ledger's validated conditions (whose thresholds the evaluation machinery then tests engine observations against), the `research_forward_assumption` that refines engine targets under the app-owned conflict policy ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6e](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6e-deterministic-target-refinement)), and the pre-profit observations (producer-dormant) — each validated before it binds, which is exactly why none of them is part of the unrestricted arm.
**Both arms are scored identically** by the outcome scoreboard ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)) — the same interval scorer under the same exclusion rules, with the head-to-head comparison computed over the **paired population only** (episodes where both arms carried the band), plus per-arm outlook direction hit-rates — so "was the model better than the baseline" is arithmetic over the same events, never opinion.
And the **prompt carries the retrospective**: the prior run's both-arm values, the price move since, and the matured scoreboard lines (a deliberate reversal of the v4 anchoring guard — self-assessment against the baseline is the model arm's point), with the model's own reflection persisting as prose input to the learnings, never as the scorekeeper.
Engine-matched caps (forensic, narrative, pre-profit) persist as **audit annotations binding the engine arm**; the conviction-raise triple and its app re-derivation are retired unbuilt (the anti-reflexivity guard survives in the ledger validation, where it feeds deterministic consumers).

### Intrinsic verdict

Decided in the per-holding loop with **no whole-book context** — it is a property of the holding alone, **and of no investor**: every intrinsic field — the disposition, grade, sub-scores, conviction, targets, horizon reads, dead-money state, and the model arm's authored values — is **profile-independent**, so the same security and evidence produce the same intrinsic record for any investor (what keeps outcome learning and cross-run comparison honest).
The investor profile reaches **no interpretation call** — the interpretation prompt does not render it (test-pinned) — so no intrinsic judgment is taken with an investor in view, and the profile shapes the prescription at the per-holding action call only ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).
What a holding's analysis resolves to is first an **outer three-arm disposition** — kept distinct so the UI and the roll-up can tell a holding that *was* analyzed from one that *can't* be (wrong asset class) and one that *shouldn't* be (too little evidence): **`not-rated`** (an ineligible class — [§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)), **`insufficient-evidence`** (eligible but below the evidence floor — the explicit abstention of [§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor), never a low-conviction guess and never a conviction *value*), or an **analyzed verdict** (the as-built `VerdictDisposition` in `portfolio/mod.rs` already carries these three arms — its `graded` arm predating the two-branch union below).
The analyzed verdict is then a **discriminated union of two branches**, and every downstream schema branches on the type — the action call's digest, the outcome episodes, and the calibration reads.
The default **`priced`** branch is the full record below.
A holding whose vehicle class is structurally unpriceable ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)) returns the **`role_risk_only`** branch instead: the **role** read (the vehicle's mandate and the exposure it exists to supply, read **in isolation** like every intrinsic field — what it does for *this* book is the future portfolio planner's whole-book judgment), the **exposure tilt**, the observable **risk** read, expense drag, the **structural flag and evidence gaps**, and the rewritten fund ledger.
It carries **no letter, no price targets, no implied-expectations read, no dead-money state, and no conviction**: none of the return-derived machinery has an input, and the engine's conviction cap rules — the raise machinery retired with `portfolio-v7` — are defined over revisions, targets, thesis drivers, and the pre-profit overlay's statement-derived execution/financing states (repeated miss, severe deterioration), priced-stock inputs this branch doesn't produce.
Its confidence surface is the **evidence gaps and degraded-input flags**, never a fabricated High / Medium / Low.
Its action comes from the same per-holding action call as the priced branch, decided from the branch's own attributes — the engine set for this branch stays the reduced {sell all, trim, hold}, rendered as evidence with the choice structurally open and departures annotated ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).
The `priced` branch:

- **Composite grade** (A–F) over the branch's applicable deterministic sub-scores: a stock supplies **quality**, **valuation**, and **risk**; a priced equity fund supplies real **valuation** and **risk**, with the structurally absent quality axis represented only by the shared neutral-50 imputation and visible low-confidence marker defined in [§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility).
  **Momentum is computed alongside but lives outside the letter**, surfaced as the market-setup read in the conviction / positioning context: a price move is not a business fundamental, and the letter is presented as *what the business is* ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
- **Conviction** — the verdict's confidence, and the one read that moves in **both** directions.
  Since `portfolio-v7` the model's conviction is **its own, unrestricted** (the two-arm contract above): the engine-matched cap producers — the **narrative-vs-reality** read (designed), the **forensic caps**, and the pre-profit ceilings — persist as **audit annotations that bind the engine arm's mechanical conviction**, render beside the model's value where it exceeds them, and never clamp it; the raise machinery (the decomposed triple, the `validated_leading_indicator` citation, the app re-derivation) is retired unbuilt.
  Below the evidence floor the holding still abstains entirely — the outer `insufficient-evidence` disposition above, never a conviction value (see [§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)).
- **Forward outlook** — the holding's *forward* read, kept distinct from the backward grade and surfaced beside it: separate short-, mid-, and long-term directional reads, together with the scenario price targets below — read against the engine's **implied-expectations range** (what the current price already assumes — [§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 2), so "strong outlook, already paid for" is a computed contrast, not a vibe.
  A grade/outlook divergence — a weak business with an improving setup, or a strong one the market has already paid for — is shown as such rather than averaged into the letter ([§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict) intro).
- **Price targets** — **one-month and twelve-month**, each a rolling window from the run date (the settled rename of the as-built end-of-month / end-of-year fields — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), in **both arms**: the engine's scenario outputs with their **methodology and assumptions exposed** (including any research-sourced forward assumption folded in post-research — see [§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 5), aligned to the ledger's bear/base/bull monitor and app-stamped there; and the model's own freely-authored bands beside them (the two-arm contract above — the monitor and the baseline's deterministic readings take the engine's alone).
- **Financial analysis** — a concise read of the company's financial health.

### Portfolio action

Decided by the dedicated **per-holding action call** after the intrinsic verdict is finished — one holding at a time, from that holding's own evidence plus the investor profile, with **no whole-book input** (the tunnel-vision contract, `portfolio-v9`; the whole-book reconciliation belongs to the future portfolio-planner job):

- **Action**, on a fixed ladder: **sell all → trim → hold → add → add aggressively** — **rung only**, with a one-line rationale persisted on the verdict.
  No target weight, share count, or dollar figure is authored — sizing is whole-book work, deferred to the planner — and no orders are ever placed.
- **The decision's evidence set.**
  The rung is decided from an explicit input set rather than a model's gestalt: both arms' **grades and scores**, the **conviction**, the **horizon outlook**, the **upside / downside** the targets imply against the current price with their typed provenance, and the engine's **capital-efficiency read**: the holding's forward base-case **total return** — price drift plus shareholder yield — against a hurdle, the run-level risk-free rate plus a **risk-tier-scaled** premium, flagging a position as **dead money** only on the hurdle's **fails** state, when even the bull case misses it (an indeterminate read flags nothing).
  It also carries the **risk tier** (assigned deterministically per branch — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), the position's own **unrealized P/L** (a harvestable loss or a taxable gain, by sign — flagged as a user consideration, never the mover), any finalized **pre-profit overlay rules**, and the prior run's action as the continuity baseline.
  The engine computes its **own per-holding feasible set** — the add family enters only when the new-money admission test passes, the hurdle isn't `fails`, the grade isn't F, and no overlay rule bars it; *add aggressively* additionally needs an A/B grade; severe deterioration restricts the whole set to the exit family — and that read reaches the model as **evidence, never a bar** (the two-arm contract, [§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)): the full ladder is open, and a chosen rung outside the engine set persists exactly as authored with the departure **app-stamped on the holding's audit** (`action_annotations`).
  Since `portfolio-v9` the set carries **no book-level term** — the retired concentration-headroom and buying-power gates were whole-book context, the planner's domain.
  A **`role_risk_only`** holding ([§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict)) decides from its own attributes — class label, role read, exposure tilt, expense drag, observable risk, structural flag, evidence gaps — and its **engine set** is **{sell all, trim, hold}**: the add family requires return evidence this branch has none of by construction, so an add-side rung must be earned by the vehicle's own merits in the rationale, persisting with the recorded annotation (user decision 2026-08-14: the full ladder is open to this branch).
- **The profile's one entry point.**
  The investor profile (objective, risk tolerance, horizon, tax posture — [configuration.md §Investor Profile](configuration.md#investor-profile)) frames the decision — an aggressive risk tolerance can justify the aggressive rung where the evidence supports it — and never changes the verdict's facts, which are authored profile-blind upstream.

  **Capital efficiency and the sunk-cost guard.**
  A holding flagged **dead money** is where the decision deliberately overrides the disposition reflex.
  The instinct to hold a deep loser "until it comes back" — or because selling "wouldn't free much cash" — treats the unrealized loss as a reason to stay; the spine treats it as the opposite.
  When the hurdle read is **fails** — even the bull case misses over the holding's horizon — **realizing the position (some or all) is leaned toward, not away from**, and two *generic* counterweights support that exit beyond the weak forward read: the **possible tax benefit** of booking the loss, and the **redeployment optionality** of the freed capital — worthwhile even when the proceeds are modest, since dead capital earns nothing.
  These are weighed **high-level**: the job models neither the user's account type and tax rate nor a specific replacement name (naming the next idea stays Trade Opportunities' job, kept isolated — [trade-opportunities.md](trade-opportunities.md)); it **assumes the user acts on the specifics appropriately**.
  Concretely, the tax counterweight enters the action's **rationale text as a flagged user consideration — it never moves the chosen rung on its own** (with account type, lots, and rates unmodeled, it has no standing as an analytical vote).
  Two disciplines keep this from becoming a license to dump every red position: the unrealized loss **never moves the grade or sub-scores** — those stay cost-basis-agnostic, a read of the business, never of the position's P/L ([§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict)), so it informs only the *action and its rationale* — and the tilt applies **only once the forward prospects are independently judged poor**.
  A loser with an intact, improving thesis is still a hold (or an add); the tilt toward realize-and-redeploy is reserved for a position the forward read has already written off.

### What changed

A structured **audit of the prior verdict**, not a bare diff.
It attributes every moved intrinsic value (grade, any sub-score, conviction, target, horizon, a re-weighted scenario, a tripped falsifier or fired trigger — for a `role_risk_only` holding, its role / exposure / risk reads and ledger conditions) old → new to an **external change** — **market data** (price / momentum / positioning), **company information** (earnings, guidance, a new filing, a fundamentals shift), or **research / narrative** (a catalyst or narrative change), tied to the specific engine input-delta entry or source-backed finding that moved — or to a **self-correction**, where the inputs did *not* materially change and the model is revising its own prior read.
The retired **action half** (a construction-era structure attributing moved-context causes against whole-book aggregates) no longer exists: with no portfolio context in the job, an action moves only because the verdict's evidence moved, and the action call's rationale explains the rung directly (`portfolio-v9`).

A self-correction is the **flagged exception** to firm-output continuity — it swings the verdict with no new facts — so it is labeled as such rather than dressed as an external move, and repeated self-corrections on one holding are a calibration signal.
The line also carries the **position delta** (new / increased / decreased / unchanged since last run; an unseen holding reads as `new`).

The fixed action ladder and grade vocabulary are load-bearing: like the report's fixed regime labels ([storage.md](storage.md)), they keep verdicts comparable across runs and prevent the model from retreating into hedged, non-comparable language.
The grade is **intrinsic** to the holding; the **action** additionally reflects the investor profile (horizon, risk tolerance, objective, tax sensitivity) through the action call — and nothing else.

## Portfolio roll-up

After the per-holding pass, the app builds a **deterministic run-level roll-up** (application layer, no model): the verdict counts by disposition, the largest single-position weight and the cash weight (descriptive reads for the page, consumed by no action logic), and **positions closed since the last run** (the exited names from the holdings diff — graded nowhere, but acknowledged rather than silently dropped).

The roll-up also carries the **run-level data-health read**, rolled up from the per-holding audits' typed target derivations plus the run's adapter outcomes: targets rate-anchored vs raw-percentile vs current-multiple carry, dispersion-floor engagements, deep-history failures, a run-wide DGS10 history gap, and the **local-context fit**.
The context-fit leg reads each chat call's `prompt_eval_count` against its `num_ctx` **and its sent prompt size** — the peak fill persisted, calls at ≥ 90% named as context pressure, and a count too small for the chars sent named as **likely front-truncation**, the signature fill alone cannot see ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable) the compression covenant).
Per-holding fail-soft is honest but silent at run level — the first live run left 43 of 44 anchor windows empty behind a clean-looking run — so the aggregate persists with the roll-up and renders as a one-line readout on the roll-up card.
It carries an **attention** state on infrastructure degradation (a deep-history failure, any multiple-carry target, a run-wide rate-history gap, any context-pressured call, any length-stopped local generation — the output-budget diagnostic from the pre-run slice); a raw-percentile fallback from genuinely thin issuer history is counted but never flagged.

**What this section no longer contains — the retired construction stage.**
Through `portfolio-v8` a whole-book construction stage followed the loop: deterministic book aggregates (concentration, overlap clusters, sector exposure, the sizing spine), a 122B synthesis reconciling each holding's standalone lean into a final action and target-weight range, a joint-feasibility solve, a divergence-cause vocabulary, and a portfolio-level view.
The stage was **removed whole by the tunnel-vision ruling** (user decision 2026-08-14): this job must not compare holdings — each action is decided from the holding alone plus the profile ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)) — and the whole-book questions the stage answered (concentration, overlap, funding, sizing, the deployment stance) move to the future **portfolio-planner** job, which will read this job's report beside the market report and Trade Opportunities.
Retained history is honored, not rewritten: runs persisted by the construction era — including its **degraded rows** (a construction-failed run's per-holding work, marked as having no book) — still list and open read-only, still excluded from every baseline read ([§Failure posture](#failure-posture)).

One stronger alternative recorded here stays **deferred, not adopted**: a deterministic allocation optimizer — the app solving every target weight, the model only explaining the result.
The estimation-error evidence (constrained and even naive allocations beating optimized ones out of sample) cut against optimizing at this data quality, and the question now belongs to the portfolio planner's design.

## Starting parameters (calibratable)

The deterministic engine runs on concrete constants, gathered as a single **calibration surface** at the top of `src-tauri/src/portfolio/engine.rs` (with the durable plan-time parameters in `portfolio/mod.rs`).
Every value below is an **MVP starting point chosen to be shadow-tuned against live runs, not frozen** — the same stance the report's thresholds take.
They are recorded here so the spec matches the engine, not to pin them.

**As-built (the single-equity slice, updated by the fund slice's engine work).**

- **Composite grade.**
  The letter reads the **momentum-free** sub-score weights **quality 0.40 / valuation 0.30 / risk 0.30** (fixed; the roll-up divides by their sum); letter cutoffs over the 0–100 composite — **A ≥ 85, B ≥ 70, C ≥ 55, D ≥ 40**, else F.
  **Momentum lives outside the letter** (the settled re-weight, landed with the fund slice's engine update) — computed alongside and re-homed to the **market-setup read** in the conviction / positioning layer, because a price move is the one grade input that isn't a business fundamental and the letter is described to the user as *what the business is*; keeping it in would also force two grades (a display letter and a gating composite), which the move dissolves.
- **Sub-scores.**
  Each a clamped linear map to 0–100 (higher = better): **quality** from net and gross margin, **valuation** from inverted P/E · P/S · P/B (a negative P/E scores low, never "cheap"), **momentum** from trailing price return (computed alongside; outside the letter — see Composite grade), **risk** (higher = safer) from realized volatility and debt/equity — where a **negative debt/equity** (negative equity — levered beyond the equity base) takes the band's floor rather than riding the inverted clamp to "maximally safe", the negative-P/E rule's mirror.
  The band constants were **shadow-tuned 2026-08-03 against run `3b21ae85`** (the first calibration dataset, its sub-score formulas first certified exact against the persisted audits — [verification/2026-08-03-grade-band-shadow-tune.md](verification/2026-08-03-grade-band-shadow-tune.md)): the v1 bands read normal large-cap-growth multiples as junk-expensive, compressing the whole book to C/D/F, so the recentered bands live in the engine's calibration surface and each audit stamps the **grade parameter version** (current `grade-v2.1`; a run decoding no stamp predates the tune and carries the v1 bands) so a recalibration — letters moving with no input change — stays recognizable to the what-changed audit and outcome-learning cohorts.
  The v2.1 stamp marks an input-semantics change, not a band move (2026-08-05, [verification/2026-08-05-piece3-value-chain-walk.md](verification/2026-08-05-piece3-value-chain-walk.md)): the P/E derive went signed, so a loss-maker's negative P/E now reaches the fixed "never cheap" valuation score it previously escaped as a `None` — bands, weights, and cutoffs are unchanged from `grade-v2`.

- **Rate / return representation (suite-wide).**
  Every rate, return, spread, premium, and haircut in the suite's engine math — and every such value it persists — is a **decimal ratio**: a FRED `percent` print (`4.5` for 4.5%) normalizes to `0.045` at the adapter seam before any engine consumption ([data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)), a threshold written `Npts` means `N ⁄ 100` (`+8pts` → `0.08`), and the ε = 1% guard reads `0.01`.
  Driver yields are ratios by construction (a per-share driver over a price), and the emerging-track hurdle `2^(12 ⁄ H) − 1` already pins returns to the same scale (`H = 12` reads `1.0` — a double), so this bullet states the one convention every formula below and Trade Opportunities' entry-gate row share, rather than leaving each consumer to convert independently ([trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
- **Scenario-target function (v2 — as-built with the fund slice).**
  The priced-stock bear / base / bull targets are a **rate-anchored forward-multiple** construction — per-share driver × scenario multiple — defined here as the single canonical function both jobs compute (Trade Opportunities applies it per archetype through the driver-override table in [trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
  The **driver ladder** picks the per-share fundamental deterministically — consensus **forward EPS** where a positive consensus exists, else consensus **forward revenue per share** (the ladder position recorded in the methodology) — and a rung is eligible only on a **finite, positive driver**: a non-positive or absent EPS consensus skips to the revenue rung (reciprocal-yield math over a non-positive driver is meaningless, and there is deliberately **no FCF rung** — the documented surface carries no forward-FCF consensus ([data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)), and a pre-profit name prices on the revenue rung, the same P-S lens Trade Opportunities' pre-profit overrides apply), while **no admissible rung** drops the holding to the evidence floor under the named **`no-admissible-driver`** reason ([§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)).
  The consensus the ladder reads is the **NTM (next-twelve-months) read**: the two nearest **coming** fiscal-year rows blend time-weighted by each row's overlap with the rolling twelve-month forward window (a single forward row keeps single-row semantics; no forward row abstains exactly as before), so a mostly-reported current fiscal year — whose consensus ≈ the trailing print — contributes only its remaining months rather than masquerading as the forward year (the first live run's flat-target mechanism, closed 2026-08-01; the rows blended and the near weight are recorded).
  Scenario values come from the consensus — **base from the mid, bear / bull from the low / high**.
  Where the analyst spread is missing the driver is held **flat** across scenarios and recorded as flat-driver on the target meta; scenario width then comes from the multiple axis and the dispersion floor, and the drafted base ∓ trailing-revision-dispersion widening waits on the revision feed.
  The implied annual growth is clamped by the v1 sanity bound (−25% / +35%), and a published spread the clamp collapses to a single value is **recorded as clamp-flattened**, distinct from a published-flat spread, so calibration data never mistakes manufactured flatness for honest consensus.
  Since `targets-v4` the clamp carries a **trough release**, gated three ways: a corroborated consensus (≥ 2 forward rows **contributing to the selected rung's blended mid** — the per-field contribution counts, never the blend count a single estimate can ride, nor mere field presence on a weightless boundary-day row), the current trailing multiple above the bounded anchor window's own rich end (P75), and the trailing print itself depressed against the window's largest admissible print (the direct trough test) — price held while the trailing print collapsed, so the clamp would crush valid recovery consensus to trough scale (attempt 2's GM: a $14.35 consensus to a $2.67 driver, a −79% "base") — whereupon the unclamped consensus prices instead, recorded as **clamp-released** and superseding the collapse record.
  The gates deliberately exclude whole-window troughs, downward re-ratings, and price-driven multiple expansion with earnings intact, where releasing would remove the sanity bound exactly when valuation is stretched; every release input is drafted, calibratable ([verification/2026-08-13-big-run-attempt-2.md](verification/2026-08-13-big-run-attempt-2.md) §Workstream 3).
  The revenue rung converts total consensus revenue per-share on **one stated share basis: the latest reported diluted share count** (the most recent filing's; where the newest filing carries no diluted count the quote-vintage shares outstanding substitutes, a fail-soft second basis — abstaining over a routinely absent field would cost more than the vintage drift).
  That is the vintage every per-share conversion in the suite reuses, Trade Opportunities' archetype overrides included ([trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
  The **scenario multiple re-anchors the driver multiple's own history on the run-level `DGS10`** — over a trailing **~3-year (12-quarter) anchor window** the engine takes the **P25 / P50 / P75 of the driver-yield spread** (driver yield − the contemporaneous `DGS10`), admitting **only anchor quarters whose trailing driver print is finite and positive** (a non-positive print is an economically invalid multiple observation — excluded, and counted against the window floor below), and maps each through today's rate, `M_s = 1 ⁄ (spread_s + DGS10_now)` — so multiples compress deterministically when the ten-year rises and expand when it falls; this is where "scenario discounting keeps `DGS10`" cashes out, the long-duration rate sensitivity living in the multiple rather than a cash-flow-by-cash-flow DCF.
  Since `targets-v4` admission also holds an **anchor-multiple sanity bound**: an observation whose raw multiple exceeds the holding's current trailing multiple by more than a drafted factor (3×) is excluded and counted as **anchor-bounded** on the target meta — a multiple that far above what the market pays today, inside a ~3-year window, is a near-zero-denominator artifact (attempt 2's RKT anchored at 420×–2,511× against a 90× current multiple) or a departed valuation regime, not reversion evidence.
  The bound is relative rather than absolute so a genuinely extreme regime the market still pays keeps its history, and a dropped observation counts against the window floor like any other inadmissible quarter — thinning to the raw fallback or the recorded multiple-carry rather than fabricating a surface.
  The anchor-window inputs are **dated, under one deterministic join**.
  Each admissible quarter anchors on its trailing driver print's **filing date** (the statement feed's; period end plus the drafted ~45-day filing grace where no filing date is carried — the freshness basis's constant, reused).
  Its driver yield reads that print against the **latest daily close on or before the anchor date** (the deep dated-EOD history already on each job's surface), and the *contemporaneous* `DGS10` is the **latest published `DGS10` observation on or before the same anchor date**.
  The full/deep path loads that history as **one date-ranged FRED request per run covering the anchor window plus alignment slack, retained as dated observations** (the acquisition rule on the DGS10 row — [data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)), while the engine-only quick paths still fetch only the run-level print they re-anchor on ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
  A failed history request leaves every spread observation inadmissible, so the window falls below the ≥ 8-observation floor and takes the documented raw-percentile fallback — no new failure state.
  The percentile→scenario assignment is **inverse in the spread domain** — `spread_bear = P75`, `spread_base = P50`, `spread_bull = P25`: a wider spread is a cheaper multiple, so the reciprocal yields `M_bear ≤ M_base ≤ M_bull` — and each scenario then pairs its own driver with its own multiple (bear × bear … bull × bull); **scenario identity comes from this mapping, never from sorting the finished prices**.
  Two guards keep the mapping honest: a **degenerate denominator** (`spread_s + DGS10_now` below a drafted ε = 1%) falls back to that scenario's raw multiple percentile, recorded; an anchor window with **fewer than 8 admissible quarterly observations** drops the rate correction entirely (raw percentiles, recorded).
  On either raw-percentile fallback the assignment **flips back to direct** — `M_bear = P25`, `M_base = P50`, `M_bull = P75` **of the raw multiples** — because the percentile rank inverts between the spread domain and the multiple domain (the cheap multiple is the bear multiple in both).
  The three prices are **monotonicity-sorted as a defensive repair only** — a residual crossing remains possible through the fallback seams (one scenario rate-anchored, another raw), and a repaired crossing is recorded — never the mechanism that assigns scenario identity.
  A **volatility-scaled dispersion floor** then guards the finished band: bear / bull are widened — never narrowed — to at least a minimum half-spread around base (drafted: annualized realized volatility × 0.5, clamped to a 5–20% half-spread, the lower bound where volatility is uncomputable), because zero scenario dispersion — a near-realized consensus, or the carry path's collapsed axes — degenerates the three-state hurdle into a point test and reads `fails` with false certainty; an engagement is recorded, and the **fund form shares the floor** (its deliberately flat driver leaves the carry path fully flat otherwise).
  The twelve-month scenario **total return** is `TR_s = (P_s + forward twelve-month dividends per share) ⁄ spot − 1` — exactly the dead-money bullet's decomposition (per-share driver growth + forward dividend yield + valuation change) computed as one function rather than three estimates, the share-count discipline holding because the drivers are per-share — and the **one-month leg keeps the v1 mechanics** (base = spot × (1 + PR_base ⁄ 12), where `PR_base = P_base ⁄ spot − 1` is the twelve-month **price-return leg** — dividends excluded, because the one-month output is a *price* target scored on the price-only label ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)) and v1's prorated `drift` is itself price-only, while `TR_base` remains the dead-money hurdle's total-return basis; volatility-scaled bands with the same fixed fallbacks).
  The function is **versioned through the calibration snapshot's parameter version** ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)), so target calibration never mixes v1 and v2 bases; the **fund form** swaps the consensus ladder for the exposure-priced composite — the settled fund-form bullet below.
- **Fund-form scenario targets (settled 2026-07-16; as-built with the fund slice).**
  A priced equity fund's bear / base / bull targets are the **v2 function applied to the fund's exposure-priced composite** ([§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)) in place of the consensus driver ladder — the same construction, guards, and fallbacks, with fund inputs swapped in.
  The **driver** is the fund's implied look-through earnings per share — `spot × composite earnings yield` (the covered-weight-renormalized `Σ(wᵢ ÷ PEᵢ) ÷ Σwᵢ` under its ≥ 70% coverage guard) — held **flat across scenarios**: no consensus low / mid / high exists for a fund, so scenario identity comes entirely from the multiple axis and the targets read as a **mix re-rating** — what the current exposure mix would be worth at cheap / median / rich pricing of that mix relative to rates — with the flat driver recorded in the methodology.
  The **anchor window** is the composite yield's own constant-current-mix trailing history (`historical-sector-pe` — the same series, exchange blend, and renormalization-and-coverage convention as the valuation read), sampled quarterly over the shared ~12-quarter window and joined to the dated `DGS10` history under the same latest-published-on-or-before join; the spread percentiles, the inverse mapping (`spread_bear = P75` … `spread_bull = P25`), the ε = 1% degenerate-denominator guard, the ≥ 8-observation floor, the direct-mapped raw-percentile fallbacks, and the recorded defensive monotonicity repair are the stock function's, unchanged.
  The twelve-month scenario **total return** adds the fund's trailing-twelve-month distributions (`dividends`) as the forward-distribution estimate — the sustainable basis a fund's payout honestly supports — and the **one-month leg keeps the v1 mechanics** (`spot × (1 + PR_base ⁄ 12)`), exactly as the stock form.
  The known **conservative bias is named**: the flat driver carries no earnings-growth leg, so `TR_base` understates a growing mix — the outcome scorecard's signed base-case error is the calibration read that sizes it ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)).
  The fund form introduces **no new floor reason**: a fund whose composite fails its coverage guard already abstains under the evidence floor's fund analog ([§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)), and a constant-mix history below the shared ≥ 8-observation floor abstains the same way — the history serves the vs-own-history valuation read too, so a window too thin to anchor is too thin to letter (the stock form's raw-percentile fallback has no fund analog).
- **Risk-tier assignment.**
  The tier the hurdle premium, the action call's digest, and the calibration snapshot consume is **assigned deterministically in the engine stage** ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6b](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6b-deterministic-financial-analysis)), per branch.
  A **priced stock** adopts Trade Opportunities' **High / Low / else-Medium rule** — [trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable) stays the canonical constants — under a stated **missing-input rule**: a leg whose input Portfolio's surface doesn't carry (event exposure rides discovery feeds this job never touches; float / liquidity is an enriching input here — [data-sources.md §Portfolio Analysis — endpoint surface](data-sources.md#portfolio-analysis--endpoint-surface)) simply **cannot trigger**, and a holding whose tier inputs are **wholesale missing** reads **Medium with a logged tier-input gap** (the neutral-imputation stance, surfaced as a degraded-input flag, never a fabricated High or Low).
  A **priced equity fund** takes its own mapping over the fund path's actually-available inputs — **High** on a leveraged / inverse structural flag, realized volatility > 40%, or maximum drawdown > 50%; **Low** on realized volatility < 25% and no structural flag; **else Medium** (drafted — the stock rule's vol / drawdown legs kept, the structural flag standing in for the balance-sheet legs a fund doesn't have; the stock rule's liquidity legs are dropped — no liquidity series is on the fund surface).
  A **`role_risk_only`** holding carries **no tier** — none of its return-linked consumers exists on that branch, whose reduced spine reads observable risk directly ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).
- **Dead-money hurdle.**
  `hurdle = rf + risk premium`, where **rf = the run-level `DGS2`** (the suite's short-end anchor, mirroring Trade Opportunities' entry-threshold anchor — a two-year yield read against an annual window, the already-fetched short rate chosen for cross-suite consistency rather than an exact duration match; scenario *discounting* keeps `DGS10` — the v2 scenario-target function's rate-anchored multiple above) and the premium is **risk-tier-scaled** — drafted **+3pts (Low) / +5pts (Medium) / +8pts (High)** — in place of a flat 5% (a high-risk holding held to a utility's premium misprices risk with the tier already computed in the spine — the assignment rule above).
  The read the hurdle tests is the holding's **base-case forward annual *total* return** — the v2 function's `TR_base` directly — whose decomposition `expected total return = per-share driver growth + forward dividend yield + valuation change` is what that function computes as one expression.
  **Share-count change appears exactly once**: the retired v1 drift used *company-level* revenue growth, so a separate net-buyback-yield term added correctly; v2's per-share drivers fold the buyback in, and the term must not also be added.
  The payout term is read **as-built from the trailing-TTM dividend sum — every dividend row in the window, specials included** (a buyback still never enters the payout leg — v2 folds it into the per-share drivers above; and FMP's dividend rows carry no special/regular flag for a clean filter to key on).
  The **forward / sustainable** payout basis — a one-off special not setting the yield — is the designed target, and until a filter lands a special inside the window inflates `TR` and can flip the hurdle / admission reads, an accepted, watched exposure.
  The hurdle verdict is **three-state, not binary**, with one exact deterministic rule over the scenario bands the engine already computes: **clears** when even the *bear-case* total return clears the hurdle; **fails** when even the *bull-case* total return misses it; **indeterminate** otherwise (a point estimate missing the hurdle inside its own scenario dispersion proves nothing).
  Only **fails** makes a holding **dead money** — a **weighed exit input** for the action decision, per the softened tilt in [§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict); **indeterminate neither tilts the decision nor creates dead money** — the scenario-dispersion tolerance is *exit-side* hysteresis; the add family runs its own admission test in the feasible-set rule below.
  The bars sit deliberately **below** Trade Opportunities' entry thresholds (rf + 8/16/30 — [trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable)): a holding may continue below the bar a fresh entry would need (hold-side hysteresis, so the book doesn't churn at the boundary); the *add* side — new dollars, which are an entry decision — is governed by the feasible-set rule below.
- **Action feasible-set bounding.**
  Before the model picks a rung, the engine narrows **its own action set** — since `portfolio-v7` prompt evidence and the annotation bound, never a schema bar — from **per-holding engine inputs only**: grade, dead-money, the new-money admission test, the hard forensic state, and any pre-profit-overlay rule, conviction being model-authored and so unable to pre-gate (`portfolio-v9`: the retired concentration and cash terms were whole-book context, the planner's domain).
  It offers *add* / *add-aggressively* only when the holding passes the **new-money admission test**: the **base-case forward total return clears the tier-scaled hurdle as a point test**.
  New dollars are an entry decision, and the three-state read's dispersion tolerance is *exit-side* hysteresis — it protects held capital, never licenses new capital; an indeterminate read whose base case clears still admits, and the test deliberately stops short of Trade Opportunities' full entry asymmetry, since an add to an owned, already-diligenced name is not a cold entry, the same rationale that sets these bars below TO's.
  Dead money (the hurdle's **fails** state) drops the family a fortiori at **any** grade — the former B-grade carve-out is removed, and *hold* stays available, the hysteresis above — as does grade = F, a tripped **hard forensic trigger** (restatement / auditor change / fraud flag — the conviction-layer caps bullet below), or a **constrained-runway / severe-deterioration** overlay state.
  It offers *add-aggressively* only for an A/B grade.
  Severe deterioration additionally restricts the engine's own action set to {trim, sell all}.
  **Every grade test in these rules reads the momentum-free letter** (the re-weight above), so a price crash alone — a momentum swing with the business unchanged — can never bar the contrarian add.
  A **`role_risk_only`** holding's engine set is **{sell all, trim, hold}** — the add family never enters it without return evidence ([§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)).
  Since `portfolio-v7` this whole rule surface is the **engine arm's** — the set renders into the action call's prompt as the engine's own read, the engine stand-in action obeys it by construction, and a model rung outside it persists with an audit annotation, never a rejection ([§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)).
  (The profile's `available_cash` is consumer-less since `portfolio-v9` — cash bounds are whole-book work, the future portfolio planner's; the field survives for the read-only Settings display and the planner.)
- **Material not-rated threshold.**
  The not-rated materiality threshold (**≥ 5%** of the account) is retained as a drafted constant with **no as-built consumer**: its consumer — the whole-book aggregates' not-rated contribution — retired with the construction stage, and the read belongs to the future portfolio planner ([§Portfolio roll-up](#portfolio-roll-up)).
- **Conviction-layer caps** (need the paid-FMP revision feed + `financial-scores`).
  Since `portfolio-v7` every cap below **binds the engine arm's mechanical conviction and rule surface and persists as an audit annotation** — the model's own conviction is unrestricted, the exceedance rendering beside the recorded rule ([§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)); the cap semantics themselves are unchanged.
  Narrative-vs-reality caps conviction when multiple expansion outruns estimate revisions by more than **~1.5×** with no leading-metric anchor; the forensic flags cap conviction — **Altman Z < 1.8**, **Piotroski ≤ 3**, **net income > 1.3× operating cash flow**, **receivables / inventory growth > 1.5× revenue growth** — with **restatement / auditor change / fraud flag the hard triggers** (the same hard set as Trade Opportunities'; the typed event producers behind all three — the item-classified filing kinds and the research-fed fraud kind, each app-validated before the hard rule fires — are single-homed at [trade-opportunities-workflow.md §Step 5c](trade-opportunities-workflow.md#step-5c-deterministic-analysis-archetype-weighted-engine)).
  A tripped soft cap is the suite's shared **categorical ceiling — the engine arm's conviction at most Medium** — applied as a plain min on the engine arm's mechanical conviction (the raise machinery is retired on **both** jobs — Portfolio's with `portfolio-v7`, Trade Opportunities' with its own two-arm contract — so neither job derives a final conviction app-side), the **soft** trigger→ceiling rule and its precedence single-homed at [trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable).
  A tripped **hard** trigger takes **Portfolio's own outcome, canonical here**: Trade Opportunities' hard outcome is exclusion at an entry gate, and an owned holding cannot be excluded, so the hard trigger translates to the strongest disposition an owned name admits.
  The verdict **stays analyzed** (the user needs an action on exactly this holding), and the **engine arm's conviction is hard-capped at Low** (the same plain-min ceiling form, strictly dominating the soft Medium ceiling — hard > soft survives the translation).
  The **add family is barred from the engine action set** ({sell all, trim, hold} — the engine-arm rule above carries the trigger; the model's own choice stays structurally open, a departure persisting with the audit annotation), and the **action decision tilts toward exit on its own merits** (the dead-money pattern — a weighed input to the model's unrestricted choice, never a bar), the letter grade untouched.
  The matched hard rule persists as an engine-arm annotation exactly as a soft ceiling does ([storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage)), recorded at [portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6g](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6g-continuity-check-and-checkpoint).
  A restatement or auditor change surfacing **between runs** arrives as a material filing through the evidence-event list below (quiet badge + research-cache invalidation + selective-run force-include) — the quick check never rewrites verdicts, so the hard outcome lands at the next full or selective pass.
  A **fraud event has no filing leg to ride between runs** — it enters only through the research-fed **`forensic_event`** producer at the holding's next full or selective pass ([trade-opportunities-workflow.md §Step 5c](trade-opportunities-workflow.md#step-5c-deterministic-analysis-archetype-weighted-engine)), a stated detection gap of the between-runs sweeps, never a silent clear.
  These move conviction and the risk read, **never the letter grade**.
  For a **thinly-covered holding** (estimates absent or stale), the narrative-vs-reality read falls back to the **operating-reality-vs-price** form — the company's own reported operating series against the move in price and multiple ([trade-opportunities.md §The two non-negotiables](trade-opportunities.md#the-two-non-negotiables)) — so the cap neither silently vanishes nor fires on noise exactly where coverage is thinnest.
- **Implied-expectations read.**
  The engine inverts its scenario math at the live price into the growth / margin trajectory the price *already assumes* — under the v2 function, the driver growth the spot implies at each scenario multiple `M_bear … M_bull`, a closed-form **range under stated assumptions** rather than one solved number (the margin dimension read where the ladder's driver is revenue-based; the shared primitive Trade Opportunities computes at Step 5c — [trade-opportunities-workflow.md §Step 5c](trade-opportunities-workflow.md#step-5c-deterministic-analysis-archetype-weighted-engine)) — computed per holding and fed to interpretation beside the forward outlook.
  It is the priced-in anchor for the job's most common real decision — trimming or keeping a winner; it informs the conviction and action read and is never itself a gate.
- **Falsifier / trigger persistence.**
  The required consecutive-observation counts (filing-cadence vs high-frequency series) and the per-condition **materiality margin** are **shared suite constants, drafted once with the contract itself** at [trade-opportunities.md §The opportunity](trade-opportunities.md#the-opportunity) — listed here as a pointer, not re-drafted; Portfolio's seams are in [§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger).
- **Fund valuation coverage.**
  A fund letter requires **≥ 70% of fund weight in P/E-usable sectors** (drafted); below the bar the valuation sub-score records as a gap and the fund abstains under the evidence floor's fund analog ([§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)).
  The composite's mechanics — covered-weight renormalization, the uncovered share reported beside the read, never averaged in, and the same exchange-blend / coverage convention on the snapshot and its history — are specified once in [§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility).
- **Quick-check attention triggers.**
  The four deterministic flag conditions, their priced-only scoping, and the clear-on-full-pass semantics are specified once at [§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only) — listed here as a pointer, not re-drafted: they carry no constants of their own beyond the shared persistence contract above.
- **Technology-event pre-flag (designed, not built — lands with its benchmark context loads).**
  The input delta flags a possible third-party repricing event — an **equity-holding read** — when the holding's **sector-relative move since the prior run** exceeds **2 × its interval-scaled realized volatility** (drafted; √-of-time scaling, the suite's cadence-honest convention), read against the same sector benchmark series the outcome labels use.
  The pre-flag only adds the conditional research topic — it asserts nothing about the cause ([§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 3).
- **Held-name research refresh lane (designed, not built — lands with the research-loop slice).**
  Every Run analysis performs a capped pre-loop current-search pass over at most **2 holdings** (drafted) whose cached research **appears reusable from the evidence available before Step 6b** or whose verdict would otherwise be carried, and only where the stored thesis ledger names a **qualitative, research-checkable key driver or falsifier**.
  The app selects one node per holding and ranks eligible holdings deterministically by **nearest dated catalyst / condition window → smallest prior-run distance to an add or exit hurdle boundary → oldest supporting-research vintage → highest priced-in-expectations strain with uncertain execution → ticker**; a holding already known from pre-loop-visible state to require a full fresh agenda is ineligible, so the lane avoids every duplicate spend it can know about when it plans.
  The later Step-6b technology-event pre-flag is deliberately **not** available to this selection: if it independently invalidates reuse after a lane slot was spent, the run logs that slot `late-invalidated`, retains its evidence as input to the now-mandatory full agenda, and does not refill the slot after the per-holding loop has begun.
  Each selected holding receives one narrow current-search topic bound to the exact stored ledger-node snapshot, with the 122B reasoner returning only **`material_update` / `no_material_change` / `unscorable`** plus source-backed claims and retrieval timestamps.
  The app validates issuer identity, node linkage, dates, and source lineage; a validated `material_update` **invalidates research reuse and force-includes the holding into a selective run's normal full loop**, while the other states change nothing.
  The lane can never confirm a falsifier, rewrite the ledger, change a target, raise or lower conviction, or choose an action; the full pass owns all interpretation, and a failed / unscorable lane attempt preserves the previous state and does not advance the holding's full-research vintage.
  The cap is deliberately fixed for the first implementation rather than user-configurable; the ordinary ~4-week full-research max age remains the liveness backstop, so the narrow lane improves daily freshness without replacing deep research.
- **Pre-profit execution / financing overlay.**
  A priced stock enters the overlay when **TTM operating income is non-positive**, or when it has **no positive forward-EPS consensus and TTM free cash flow is negative**; funds and `role_risk_only` holdings never enter it.
  The eligibility read is computed — and its result persisted — for **every priced stock**, as a three-state record: **eligible** (with the arm reasons), **not eligible**, or **not entered — inputs missing** (naming which eligibility arms were uncomputable, each a recorded gap — a missing input never infers an entry); a satisfied arm admits even while the other arm's inputs are missing, and the derived financing / execution / dilution states ride every record but are **meaningful only on an eligible read** — a non-entered record carries their neutral / unscorable defaults (the persisted shape in [storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage)).
  The structured leg computes from comparable quarterly statements: **liquid resources = cash and cash equivalents + short-term investments** (an absent short-term-investments line reads zero — only absent cash is a recorded gap); **TTM cash burn = max(0, −TTM free cash flow)**; **runway months = 12 × liquid resources ÷ TTM cash burn** (`not_burning` when burn is zero, `unscorable` when a required input is absent); TTM capex intensity; split-adjusted year-over-year diluted-share change; the latest two-quarter average gross margin; and its change from the preceding two-quarter average.
  Financing state is drafted as **not_burning** when TTM cash burn is zero, **adequate** at ≥ 24 months, **watch** at 12–<24 months, **constrained** below 12 months, and **unscorable** where runway cannot be computed.
  The research leg contributes only app-validated typed observations — `{metric kind, observation role (actual / guidance low / guidance high / point guidance / contextual level), polarity (higher-is-better / lower-is-better / target-band), numeric value, units, period, issuer scope, source URL, published at, confidence}` — over production, deliveries, bookings / backlog / reservations, guidance, and unit economics; the app merges them into a period-keyed history and the engine, not the model, computes attainment and comparable-period changes.
  On the first overlay-eligible full pass, and on any later full pass where a previously used guidance metric still has fewer than four comparable stored periods, the pre-profit research topic must make a **bounded backfill attempt over the latest four reported periods** for the issuer's principal guided operating metric or metrics.
  The attempt is an obligation to search, not to produce a row: it records the periods and sources checked plus `complete` / `partial` / `unscorable`, and missing or incomparable history remains a typed gap rather than an inferred observation.
  The engine's **guidance lower bound** is the stated low for a range or the stated value for point guidance.
  The execution-miss rule applies only to a **higher-is-better metric with a finite positive bound**; other polarities remain sourced context unless a future calibrated rule defines their attainment direction.
  A comparable actual is an **execution miss** only when it is at least **5% below** the guidance lower bound, drafted as `(bound − actual) ÷ bound ≥ 0.05`; a smaller shortfall is in-line noise, not a miss.
  **Repeated miss** means misses in at least two **distinct reported periods for the same normalized metric identity, issuer scope / perimeter, and units** among that metric's latest four comparable periods; two different metrics in one period never count twice and misses from different metrics never combine.
  **Material single miss** means the latest comparable actual for that same metric is at least **20% below** the bound (drafted).
  **Economics deterioration** means the latest two-quarter average gross margin is non-positive and at least 5 percentage points below the preceding two-quarter average; **material dilution** means split-adjusted diluted shares rose at least 15% year over year (both drafted).
  **Severe deterioration** requires at least two independent legs among repeated-or-material execution miss, constrained runway, economics deterioration, and material dilution, with at least one execution-miss or economics leg; financing plus dilution alone cannot manufacture it.
  Since `portfolio-v7` the consequences **bind the engine arm and annotate the model's** (the two-arm contract — [§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)): a repeated execution miss holds the engine arm's conviction at **Medium**, constrained runway drops the add family from the engine set, and severe deterioration holds the engine arm at **Low** and narrows the engine's action set to the exit family **{trim, sell all}** — each matched rule persisting on the audit and rendering into the prompts as the engine's own binding, while the model's conviction and action stay unrestricted with any departure recorded beside the rule.
  These consequences never change the letter grade, and no single production, delivery, burn, margin, or dilution metric can force a sale.
  Every eligibility result, input, typed observation, derived state, matched rule, and unscorable gap persists for audit and outcome calibration.
- **Evidence events.**
  The **evidence-event list** — referenced by the research-cache invalidation, the selective-run force-include, and the quick check's informational badge — is defined once.
  A **new earnings actual** is a fresh `earnings` surprise row since the last full pass, re-pulled per stock by the quick check, so the leg is checkable between full runs.
  A **material filing** is a new 8-K / 10-Q / 10-K on the EDGAR sweep, CIK-gated: a symbol whose CIK doesn't resolve reads the filing family `unknown`, which force-includes through the degraded-sweep rule rather than silently skipping the leg ([data-sources.md §SEC EDGAR](data-sources.md#sec-edgar), [§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
  A **large revision move** is drafted as the **NTM blended consensus EPS** (the same two-row forward blend the target function reads — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) moving **more than ~5%**, the percentage read only where the consensus is **positive and at least ~$0.10/share** (the denominator floor; a negative or below-floor consensus tests an **absolute move of ~$0.10/share or more** instead — both constants drafted — so the ratio can't explode into a noisy trigger), or estimate-revision breadth flipping sign, since the last full pass.
  The consensus-EPS legs read from the quick check's lightweight **revision preflight** (a per-stock `analyst-estimates` snapshot compared against the stored prior, the same NTM series the engine already snapshots as the run's consensus comparator).
  The **breadth-flip sub-leg is specified but undetectable as-built** — the preflight snapshot carries no revision-breadth read, so the sub-leg stays inert until a breadth read joins the snapshot rather than silently counting as covered.
  A **qualifying structured-news seed** is a fresh symbol-scoped `news/stock` item arriving while the holding carries a **standing technology-class falsifier** — a known threat axis plus new news on it: the deterministic conjunction, deliberately **high-recall and not topic-matched**.
  Any fresh news on a tech-flagged holding qualifies, since deterministic headline-to-threat-axis matching would be fabricated precision, and the cost of an occasional spurious force-include is bounded by how few holdings carry a standing technology falsifier — the quick check pulls `news/stock` only for those holdings.
  An announcement with **no price impact and no standing falsifier** is deliberately outside the list, because a headline's materiality can't be classified without a model and a fabricated gate would be worse than the honest boundary — the next full pass's fresh seeds catch it, bounded by the ~4-week max-age.
  So every active leg of the list is deterministically detectable by the pass that promises it (the breadth-flip sub-leg above being the one recorded inert exception).
  Those legs are **equity legs** — the estimate, earnings, and news-seed series are company-scoped.
  A **fund** holding carries its own deterministic legs, detected from the quick check's **fund refresh**: a **material `etf/info` change** — the reported expense ratio moving, or the asset-class / mandate fields that route its strategy classification changing (a structural-flag reclassification, a vehicle newly reading leveraged / inverse, counts here) — for every fund, and, per **equity** fund on either verdict branch (the guard crossing flips the branch in **both** directions — a demotion below it or a promotion above it), an **exposure shift**: the US share crossing the ≥ 70% US-exposure guard, or a top sector weight moving **~10 points or more** (drafted).
  Each fund comparison is **independently gated on what each side actually derives from**: the mandate / asset-class fields, the strategy label, and the structural (leveraged / inverse / overlay) flag each compare only where **both** the stored basis and the fresh read actually carry that field — a basis persisted before the structural flag existed **degrades that comparison to no-read rather than fabricating a change** — blank `etf/info` metadata strings normalize to absent at the adapter (so blank → value reads as data coverage arriving, never as a mandate change), and a degraded fund refresh types the family **`unknown`**, never a change.
  Fund legs invalidate research reuse and force-include in a selective run exactly as the equity legs do — alongside, not instead of, the model-authored fund-ledger conditions, which reach a selective run through the ordinary flag-based force-include.
- **Research reuse window (designed — nothing is cached while the research stage is stubbed).**
  A holding **reuses its cached research** (the loop and distillation skip; interpretation reads the cached distilled findings) when that research is younger than **~4 weeks** — aligned with the suite's research-freshness / forward-assumption credit window ([trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — *and* nothing moved: position unchanged, no attention-trigger condition, no event pre-flag, **no new evidence event since the research date, and no validated material update from the held-name refresh lane** — any evidence-event leg (all visible to 6a/6b even on a reuse pass) or material refresh result **invalidates the cache**, since price and trigger movement alone would miss exactly the events research exists to read.
  Past the window the full agenda is mandatory, so the window doubles as the max-age service level; every reuse decision is logged in the audit record.
- **Resume window (designed — rides the checkpoint/resume path).**
  An interrupted run offers **resume** only while its pinned holdings pull is younger than **~48 hours** (drafted); past the bound the checkpoints are stale against a book that may have moved, so Run analysis starts a new run ([§Failure posture](#failure-posture)).
- **Rate-cache max age (quick paths).**
  The engine-only quick paths' cached-print fallback for a failed rate retrieval ([§Failure posture](#failure-posture)) is eligible only while the cached print is younger than **~1 week** (drafted — reusing the house-view freshness bound); older, or no cache at all, types the rate-dependent families **`unknown`** rather than computing off a stale anchor ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only); Trade Opportunities' Quick Audit shares the rule — [trade-opportunities-workflow.md §ATO: the audit flow](trade-opportunities-workflow.md#ato-the-audit-flow)).
- **Outcome learning (constants).**
  The full contract — episode creation and vintage stamping, branch typing, `action_source`, label mechanics, and the derived reads — lives in [§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration).
  Its calibratable constants start with the **matured archive's cap** — drafted **5,000** rows, evicted oldest-first.
  The active store carries no cap: an episode still accruing labels is never evicted; it is **age-bounded rather than count-capped** — an episode matures out into the archive once its 12-month labels record, normally right at 12 months.
  One held **pending** by a price-coverage gap stays active only through the shared **price-coverage grace** — drafted **~3 months past the window end**, the constant homed here and shared with Trade Opportunities — then closes typed: **`price-coverage-unscorable`** on a symbol the source never covered, the terminal contract on a covered series that stopped.
  So an episode is never matured from stale bars and never pending forever — the closure rule in [storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage), the label contract in [§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration) — and the active set holds roughly a year of decision changes on the book.
  The next constant is the **proposal eligibility bar** — **≥ 30 unique holdings with matured windows**, clustered by holding, never raw episode counts (each proposal's uncertainty interval clusters by **holding × decision date**), a slice-specific proposal needing its own cohort clearing the same bar.
  Last is the bear–bull band's declared **nominal coverage of 80%** (drafted), scored with an interval score on the price-only label.
- **Grade normalization & inputs (full Portfolio).**
  When the full slice lands, the sub-score inputs inherit the shared engine's Trade-Opportunities-side machinery rather than staying raw clamped maps: **quality** lifts the value-creation reads — **gross profitability (GP / assets)**, the **ROIC-vs-cost-of-capital spread**, and **FCF conversion** — rather than resting on margins alone, and every factor normalizes against **sector-adjusted absolute bands + the name's own history** (the Step-5c basis) with **sector-appropriate metric selection** — P/B-family valuation for financials, FFO-style economics for REITs, debt/equity dropped where it is structurally meaningless (banks) — because universal margin / P-E / D-E bands misgrade banks, REITs, cyclicals, and negative-equity names; the weights and letter cutoffs recalibrate then.
- **Forward-conviction raise — retired with `portfolio-v7` (unbuilt).**
  The designed research raise — at most one band, citing a validated *engine-unscored* leading indicator (the typed **`validated_leading_indicator`** — [portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6d](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6d-distillation)), 6g-validated — is retired with the two-arm unrestriction: conviction is the model's own ([§The holding verdict](#the-holding-verdict)), and the anti-reflexivity guard survives only where it feeds deterministic consumers, the ledger's tripped/fired validation ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6g](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6g-continuity-check-and-checkpoint)).
  The typed indicator still reaches the model as ledger-driver evidence when the research loop lands.
  Trade Opportunities retired the same machinery on the same reasoning when it adopted its own two-arm contract, so the retirement is now suite-wide; its **cap** contract — the shared soft ceiling, and its own hard-trigger exclusion — stands, binding that job's engine arm ([trade-opportunities.md §The opportunity](trade-opportunities.md#the-opportunity)).
- **Whole-book reads — deferred to the planner.**
  Correlation / overlap co-sizing and the dedicated **factor-exposure model** are the future portfolio planner's, deferred beside the rest of the whole-book machinery ([§Portfolio roll-up](#portfolio-roll-up)); the construction stage's drafted thresholds retired with it (above), for the planner to re-draft.
  That deferred factor model is also the **named home for portfolio stress reads** — rate / credit / commodity / dollar shock scenarios and marginal risk contribution, the tail co-movement a short-window return correlation can't see — deferred with it, not separately scheduled.

## Continuity and isolation

The job retains its most recent N runs.
The prior run feeds the next (continuity above), and what this job vectorizes lands in the **Portfolio Analysis memory partition only** — as-built the matured outcome learnings are the partition's sole write, the per-holding summary embeddings and their Step-6a semantic retrieval designed, not built ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6a](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6a-dossier-assembly)) — its learnings are never read by the report or by Trade Opportunities, and it never reads theirs (see [local-models.md §Run history and continuity](local-models.md#run-history-and-continuity)).
This keeps holding-grading calibration cumulative within the job and uncontaminated by other jobs' context.

## Outcome learning (calibration)

The calibration stance below ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — every constant an MVP starting point, shadow-tuned against live runs — needs something to tune *against*: a recorded, engine-computed read of how the job's own past calls actually resolved.
Trade Opportunities carries this machinery already ([trade-opportunities.md §Outcome learning](trade-opportunities.md#outcome-learning-calibration)); Portfolio's analog is structurally simpler — the universe is the holdings list, so there is no shadow ledger of turned-away names — but adds one confound of its own: **the user acts on the book between runs**, so what happened to a position reflects both the job's call and the user's response.

**A decision episode is created when a holding's recommendation state changes** — the verdict branch, the action, or the standing thesis — anchored on the run date (`portfolio-v9`: the action IS the whole decision, so the construction-era legs — lean-vs-final splits and target-weight-range moves — are retired from the creation rule with their producers).
The standing thesis counts as *changed* only when the run's what-changed audit records an attributed intrinsic move or a labeled self-correction for the holding — a wording-only rewrite over an empty audit line is a re-affirmation, so model rephrasing can't mint episodes; **input movement alone never opens an episode** (rates, prices, and sub-scores — and the scenario targets recomputed off them — move every run: a re-affirmed decision over moved inputs is still one decision, the movement recorded as an observation on the active episode), and a run that merely **re-affirms** *extends* the holding's active episode (the re-affirmation noted, no new anchor), so weekly runs can't manufacture overlapping pseudo-observations of a single decision.
(As-built the standing-thesis leg is dormant: the 6g what-changed attribution validator it keys on is not yet built, so episodes open only on the observable state changes — a verdict-branch flip or an action change — a conservative narrowing that can miss a thesis-only pivot but can never mint a false episode.)
Across an **abstention** the comparison runs against the **standing episode's own** recorded action, not the abstained verdict's — an `insufficient-evidence` verdict re-authors none, so comparing against it could see only the branch, and a recommendation that moved across the abstention extended the episode it had just superseded (the new forecast accruing onto the old one's window, calibration scoring a decision against observations that postdate it).
The extension rule applies only **while an episode is active**: an episode is a bounded twelve-month measurement instrument, not a live-state mirror, so once a holding's episode matures, its unchanged recommendation legally carries **no active episode** — a post-maturity re-affirmation records nothing (there is no open forecast left to observe against), and the next genuine state change opens a fresh episode under the creation rule above — the Portfolio form of Trade Opportunities' one-open-episode-per-live-lifecycle bound ([trade-opportunities.md §Starting parameters](trade-opportunities.md#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
The two halves keep **separate clocks inside the one episode stream**: besides its anchor date, each episode stamps its **intrinsic vintage** — the run that actually *authored* the intrinsic fields it records.
On a full pass the two coincide; on a **selective run** a rule-demoted carried action ([§Triggering](#triggering)) can open an episode whose intrinsic fields keep their **older vintage**, and the **intrinsic calibration reads — the action-keyed cohorts and target calibration — consume only episodes whose intrinsic vintage equals their anchor** (a fresh forecast, scored from the date it was made; the carried fields' forecast is already scored by the episode that authored them).
Which episode is "the latest" — for extension, for a confirmed falsifier crossing, and for the sector identity a carried open inherits — is **insertion order**, matching run identity across the whole suite; `anchor_at` is the episode's dated anchor, never its identity.
Selected by the wall clock, a backwards clock step would place a newly opened episode before an older active one and shadow it permanently, every later observation and event attaching to the stale predecessor while the episode carrying the current recommendation accrued nothing for the rest of its twelve months.
The matured-archive cap prunes on the same key, so a stepped clock cannot delete the just-matured row ahead of an older one.
Episodes are **typed by verdict branch, with an explicit schema per branch**.
A **`priced`** episode records the action — since `portfolio-v9` the recorded `lean` equals it and the divergence field stays `None`, the pair retained on the schema so construction-era episodes (whose lean, divergence stamps, and decided weight ranges are real recorded history) keep decoding and scoring unchanged.
Episode *identity* compares the action alone; the retired ledger weight range no longer exists to compare.
It records the grade, the conviction (the single categorical value — the decomposed triple is retired with `portfolio-v7`), the scenario bands and base-case target, the dead-money state, the position delta, and the degraded-input flags.
It records a compact **calibration-feature snapshot** — the decision-time engine inputs a future parameter proposal needs for counterfactual re-testing: the sub-scores (the composite re-derives under the stamped parameter version's shared weights), the risk tier, the scenario total-return distribution, the hurdle inputs (the DGS2 print, the tier-scaled hurdle rate, and the three-state read), and the cap signals in force (as-built the pre-profit overlay's matched rules — the narrative-vs-reality and forensic cap producers are designed, their signals joining the snapshot when they land).
The snapshot also carries the **authoring-time spot** the targets were priced from (the basis bridge the split-safe scoring below reads across), the **entry-stamped sector identity** (the sector label at the anchor run + its resolved SPDR benchmark symbol — the relative labels' benchmark carrier, per [the measurement contract below](#outcome-learning-calibration)), and the **parameter version** the decision ran under.
That is because the run's own audit record can age out of the 30-run retention before a 12-month label matures, so the episode carries what re-testing needs rather than pointing at a run that may no longer exist.
A **`role_risk_only`** episode ([§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict)) records only the action, the position delta, the degraded-input flags, and the entry-stamped sector identity where one resolves (a multi-sector vehicle carries the typed `sector-unscorable` state — the measurement contract below) — its snapshot's reduced analog, since no grade, conviction, band, target, or dead-money field exists on its verdict to record.
It is **excluded from target calibration and every grade-linked read**, and is counted **in its own class, never pooled** with priced episodes in the action-cohort spreads.
It carries **no parameter version**, deliberately: every read keyed on one is a grade-linked or target-calibration read, and this branch is excluded from all of them, so the field would have no possible consumer.
Each episode also carries an **`action_source`** field — `model-chosen` or `rule-demoted` — because a stale-action demotion ([§Triggering](#triggering)) is not an analytical decision: rule-demoted actions stay out of the pooled cohort reads (their own class), so the hold cohort measures only holds a model actually chose.
The construction-era restore path (a validated context trim moving a demoted rung back to `model-chosen`) retired with that stage, so the rule-demoted class holds only actions a rule actually set.
The next run's deterministic holdings diff then tags the holding's active episode — where one exists — with **`observed_net_alignment`** — **aligned / contrary / partial / unknown / reversed** — from the net quantity move relative to the recommended direction (`hold` reads aligned when unchanged; a net **long↔short reversal always tags `reversed`**, never `aligned` — crossing zero lies outside any long-side recommendation's direction, so the tag is its own class, excluded from the aligned / contrary cohort slices, while the episode's labels keep maturing on the name's path exactly as after an exit — [§Asset eligibility](#asset-eligibility)).
The name is deliberate: a net diff cannot see round trips, transfers, partial execution, or a user acting twice between runs, so the tag claims only what the diff can observe — never that advice was "followed."
Episodes persist in their own store, **independent of the 30-run retention** (a count-based retention can age out before a 12-month label matures); a matured episode is **frozen into a compact matured archive** once its 12-month labels record — not discarded at maturation, because matured episode-level rows are what let a future parameter proposal be tested against realized history and let uncertainty intervals be recomputed; an embedding or aggregate summary cannot recreate them ([storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage)).

**Confirmed falsifier events.**
A priced episode also records each confirmed falsifier that attaches while the episode carries the condition as `{condition_id, confirmed_at, confirmation_observation_id}`.
The event lives with the episode rather than only in a run audit that can age out before the twelve-month read matures; a falsifier that confirms **after the holding's episode has matured** (the recommendation unchanged, so no active episode to attach to — [the episode boundary above](#outcome-learning-calibration)) records on the holding's **latest matured episode instead, typed `post_maturity`** — retained as context for the next episode, though it feeds no lead-time read, that read being bounded to the matured episode's own window; `role_risk_only` episodes retain their ordinary condition-evaluation state but carry no price-derived lead-time record.

**The labels are engine-computed, never model-judged**, under the measurement contract Trade Opportunities pinned: forward return at **1 / 3 / 6 / 12 months** — absolute and vs sector / market — plus maximum drawdown, reconstructed statelessly from FMP dated-EOD daily bars.
**Before a window is marked matured**, the outcome pass refreshes that episode's symbol-scoped dated-EOD series through the window end via the shared price-bar cache — independently of the current holdings work-list, so an exited name refreshes too.
The one fetch per symbol per pass ranges from the **symbol's earliest active-episode anchor** (the benchmark series included), so every active episode's windows land on a single adjustment basis and an older-anchored episode is never left with a start the pass's one fetch cannot heal.
A holding's own series additionally floors at its **intrinsic vintage** where that is older than the anchor, which a rule-demotion open makes routine: the vintage is the session the basis bridge keys at, and a floor stopping at the anchor left the bridge's own bar outside the refreshed range.
Because a merge rewrites only the dates it fetched and the bar cache is never pruned, a bar cached before a split then satisfied the bridge on a **stale adjustment basis** instead of being excluded — fabricating a material-drawdown breach off a pre-split price.
Excluded-not-guessed still governs the case the source genuinely cannot serve that session.
A failed refresh may use the cache only when its latest bar reaches the window end; otherwise the label stays **pending** with a price-coverage gap rather than maturing from stale bars (the sector / market benchmark legs follow the same coverage rule).
A leg still pending past the shared **price-coverage grace** ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable); the closure rule single-homed in [storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage)) closes typed — **`price-coverage-unscorable`** where the source never covered the symbol, the terminal contract where a covered series stopped — counted and logged, excluded from spreads and denominators, so a pending leg can't hold its episode active forever.
The entry reference is the **next session's close** after the run, the run instant dated to its **ET session day** — never the UTC date, which an evening-ET run has already rolled into, keying the entry one session late and the basis bridge to a session traded entirely after the decision — (a consistent evaluation anchor, deliberately not called an executable price); returns are split-adjusted; and terminal events are **typed and scored per type** — an acquisition realizes at the final trading price, a bankruptcy scores to zero, an ambiguous delisting is `terminal-unscorable`, excluded from spreads but counted and logged.
(As-built no corporate-action feed records terminal facts, so a previously covered series that stops resolving conservatively closes `terminal-unscorable` past the grace — the acquisition- and bankruptcy-typed scoring activates when a corporate-action fact source lands.)
One deliberate Portfolio divergence from that contract: labels are computed on **two declared bases, with total return primary** — the decision rules being calibrated (the dead-money hurdle, the action rules) are themselves total-return rules, and scoring them price-only would mismeasure exactly the dividend-heavy names the total-return fix exists for — while the **price-only read is retained as the cross-entry common basis and diagnostic** (derivable for every entry; any comparison with a missing total-return leg quotes price-only, a labeled mix still being a mix).
The two bases have **defined mechanics**: the price leg reconstructs from the split-adjusted dated-EOD bars alone (the shared cache is price-only — [storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage)); the total-return leg adds the window's cash dividends from a **label-time `dividends` re-pull** on the episode's symbol — symbol-scoped, so an exited name is as computable as a held one — summed **without reinvestment** over the anchor price (a declared simplification, immaterial at these windows; a failed re-pull records that episode's total-return leg unavailable, taking the labeled price-only fallback above).
  The dividend window is bounded on the **bars either side of it, not the calendar window** — strictly after the entry bar's own session (an ex-date on the entry session is already out of the entry price) and up to and including the **end bar's** session, which is the last close at or before the window end.
  A window end falling on a weekend, a holiday or a stale cache tail leaves days between that close and the calendar bound, and a dividend going ex inside them is not yet out of the price the label divides by — counting it overstated the return, always in the same direction, since dividends only add.
And the **relative legs — vs sector / market — run on the price-only common basis on both sides**: the benchmark series carry no distributions, so a total-return holding leg against a price-only benchmark would bias every spread by roughly the dividend yield; **total-return-primary is the absolute label**, the one the hurdle and action rules calibrate against.
The sector leg's benchmark identity is the episode's **entry-stamped sector identity** — stamped at the anchor run from the per-stock `profile` sector read the fresh pass fetches (one fail-soft FMP call per fresh-passed stock, added with the outcome-learning slice), resolvable after an exit, never a label-time re-classification; a holding with no valid sector mapping carries the typed **`sector-unscorable`** state on its sector legs, the market leg unaffected ([trade-opportunities.md §Outcome learning](trade-opportunities.md#outcome-learning-calibration) — the shared measurement contract's canonical rule).
A user's exit does **not** stop measurement: the label tracks the *name's* path from the decision date whether or not the position is still held — that is exactly what makes a sell call scorable — with the alignment tag carrying the confound into the reads.

Four derived reads; where a read scores returns, it is computed per matured window on **unique-holding counts**, sliceable by the alignment tag, and on an **explicitly declared return basis** (the basis follows the rule being scored, never a blanket default) — the alignment-tag slicing and the legacy divergence strata below are **recorded, not yet derived**: as-built `derive_reads` slices by neither (both persist per episode, so the strata stay retroactively computable, landing with the calibration-proposal slice):

- **Action-cohort spreads** — did the add-cohort outperform the hold-cohort, and the hold-cohort the trim / sell-cohort?
  Cohorts form on **two declared layers, matched to what each calibrates**.
  For **intrinsic** parameters (sub-score weights, the hurdle, the caps) the cohort key is the recorded **action** — since `portfolio-v9` the decision itself — read from **vintage-fresh episodes only** ([the episode boundary above](#outcome-learning-calibration)).
  The vintage-fresh restriction survives from the construction era for a still-live reason: a carried action's forecast was already scored by the episode that authored it.
  The construction-era **final-action strata** (stratified by divergence-from-lean rationale) survive as recorded legacy data on old episodes; on tunnel-vision episodes the two layers coincide by construction.
  `role_risk_only` episodes stay in their own class as before.
  Computed on the **absolute total-return labels as the primary ordering read** — the ladder orders total-return expectations, so the read's basis must match the rule being scored (and the benchmark's missing dividend leg is largely common to the compared cohorts at matched windows, so it nets out of a spread anyway) — with the **sector / market-relative price-only spreads retained as the regime-controlled diagnostic** (absolute cross-cohort comparisons are confounded by *when* the decisions were made, which is exactly what the relative leg controls for).
  The Portfolio form of Trade Opportunities' picked-vs-rejected spread: the cleanest read of whether the action ladder is ordering names correctly.
- **Target calibration** — band coverage against its **declared nominal level** (drafted **80%** — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), scored **on the price-only label**: the bands and base case are *price* targets, so the price path is the only honest comparison — a total-return read would systematically overshoot the band for dividend payers.
  It uses an **interval score** that evaluates calibration and sharpness together, since raw hit-rate rewards an arbitrarily wide band and a proper scoring rule can't be gamed by width.
  Each band is scored at its **matching window**: the one-month band against the 1-month label, the twelve-month band against the 12-month label (the 3- and 6-month labels serve the cohort reads, not band scoring).
  Each is read from a **vintage-fresh episode's recorded band** — a band an extending run merely recomputes rides as an observation, not a new forecast — so every scored band anchors on the date it was authored.
  Beside coverage rides the signed error of the base case: a systematic-bias read on the scenario engine (and, indirectly, on the scenario-target function the dead-money read consumes).
  Band comparison runs **in return space from the decision instant**, bridged by the label-time series' own **anchor-session close** — the last close at or before the episode's **intrinsic-vintage ET session**, the session the authoring spot and targets belong to, the anchor session itself on the vintage-fresh episodes this read admits, bounded to the same few-day session proximity as the entry (a sparse cache's years-old pre-anchor bar is no decision-instant close).
  Band edges read as `edge ⁄ authoring-spot − 1` against the realized `end ⁄ anchor-close − 1`, the signed base-case error reconstructing the realized price in the authoring basis the same way.
  That is because the authored band is an absolute price in its authoring-time basis while label-time closes are retroactively split-adjusted, and both bridge legs must share the decision instant — the next-session entry sits an overnight gap away, which would shear the comparison even without a split.
  The residual is intraday quote-vs-close, and the return-space interval score is additionally comparable across price levels.
  The read is computed **per target-parameter version** (the never-mix rule cashing out — [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)), and an episode with no recorded authoring spot — or a window with no anchor-session bar inside that bound — is excluded rather than compared across bases.
- **Falsifier lead-time** — **priced episodes only**, using fields already frozen at the episode's intrinsic vintage: the recorded **twelve-month bear-case target is the material-drawdown line**, and that target's rolling **twelve-month window is the thesis-realization window** for this read.
  A confirmed falsifier event attaches to the episode that carried the condition as `{condition_id, confirmed_at, confirmation_observation_id}`; the engine records the **signed trading-day distance** from `confirmed_at` to the first within-window close below the bear-case line — positive when the falsifier confirmed before the breach, zero on the same session, negative when the line had already broken before confirmation — or explicit **`no-material-drawdown`** when no such close occurs by maturity.
  `confirmed_at` is the **confirming pass's** ET session date — the session whose print pushed the streak to its required count, carried on the crossing from the condition's own evaluation state.
  Deliberately not the pass that *consumes* the crossing: a between-run sweep can confirm days before the next full run reads it, so dating it at the consumer understated every lead time by the whole sweep-to-run gap and could inverse-sign a falsifier that genuinely led its drawdown.
  A UTC-dated evening confirmation would sit one session late in the bar positioning for the same reason, a day rather than a gap.
  Each standing confirmation records **one** event: the observation that re-raises an unconsumed breach changes every pass by design (it re-raises against the newest print until 6g acknowledges it), so the event is keyed on the confirmation, not on that moving observation.
  The line is evaluated as its **label-basis equivalent through the same anchor-close bridge** — a close breaches when it falls below `anchor-close × bear ⁄ authoring-spot` — so neither a split nor the overnight gap into the entry anchor can fabricate or hide a breach.
  The bridge keys at the episode's **intrinsic vintage** — the session the authoring spot belongs to, older than the episode anchor on a rule-demotion open — because this read, unlike band calibration, admits vintage-stale episodes, and an anchor-keyed bridge would shear the line by the intrinsic-to-anchor move.
  An episode with no recorded authoring spot or no bridge-session bar at that vintage (legacy pre-field episodes; a start-uncovered series; an intrinsic session before the symbol's fetch floor) leaves its falsifier events **unstamped and excluded from the read** — never compared across bases.
  This makes “early enough to matter” deterministic rather than interpretive; `role_risk_only` episodes are excluded because they carry no price band.
- **Self-correction accumulation** — the per-holding self-correction counts the what-changed audit already labels ([§What changed](#what-changed)), made cumulative: repeated self-corrections on one holding are the calibration signal that clause names, now actually counted.
  (As-built dormant beside the creation rule's thesis leg: no attribution validator labels self-corrections yet, so the counters stay structurally zero until it lands.)

**The two-arm scoreboard (`portfolio-v7`).**
A priced episode's calibration snapshot additionally freezes the **model arm at open** — its target bands, sub-scores, and outlook, beside the engine stand-in's outlook / conviction / action — so both arms stay scorable after the run ages out (a pre-v7 episode carries neither and drops out of the model-arm reads alone).
The band calibration above scores the model's frozen bands with the same interval scorer under the same exclusion rules (`model_target_calibration`, an inverted authored pair read as `(min, max)`) — each arm's read over that arm's own full band population — while the **head-to-head comparison is computed separately over the paired population alone** (`head_to_head`: episodes where **both** arms carried the band and the window scored), both arms scoring the same realized outcomes, so the comparison is same-events by construction and can never be skewed by one arm's easier population; an **outlook direction hit-rate** scores each arm's horizon reads against the realized price-only sign at mapped windows — short → 1-month, mid → 6-month, long → 12-month — with neutral reads counted beside the hit-rate, never inside it, and a zero realized return scoring a directional call as a miss.
The model's sub-scores and conviction are recorded on the snapshot but not yet scored — a predictor-quality read needs the eligibility bar below, and sub-scores have no per-episode ground truth — and the matured-note learning text gains the **paired** model-vs-engine interval comparison per window, so the memory partition and the roll-up scoreboard carry the arithmetic verdict, never the model's self-report — the retrospective prompt carries the holding's own deterministic matured window lines ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 6f](portfolio-workflow.md#step-6f-interpretation-and-grading)).
The reads render on the roll-up as the **model-vs-engine scoreboard** line set, pending states until v7 episodes mature.

Two disciplines keep the reads honest.
**Attribution stays at the cohort level** — a single trim that preceded a rally was a risk decision, not an error, so no per-decision P&L verdict is ever assigned; only cohort spreads and calibration rates feed anything.
And the loop is **propose-only, on the same stance as Trade Opportunities**: realized outcomes *propose* sub-score-weight / hurdle / cap changes for the user's review, never auto-apply — intrinsic-parameter proposals reading the **action-keyed cohorts** ([the spreads read above](#outcome-learning-calibration)) — and no proposal is made below the eligibility bar (drafted in [§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable): **≥ 30 unique holdings with matured windows**, clustered by holding, never raw episode counts — each proposal reporting its effect size and an uncertainty interval clustered by **holding *and* decision date**, since names judged in the same market regime share shocks).
(As-built each run records the typed below-bar eligibility note; the proposal statistics themselves — effect sizes with clustered uncertainty intervals — land with a later slice once matured data exists to exercise them.)
The bar is a *minimum*: a **slice-specific** proposal — per-sector, per-action-rung, per-parameter — needs its own cohort clearing the same bar, not a share of the pooled one.
On a single book of a few dozen names all of this accrues slowly — deliberately: a small sample proposes nothing rather than proposing noise.

This scorecard is also the **named graduation path for the held-out signals**: the positioning layer and the Schwab options-activity signal enter the sub-scores only when these reads show they add discriminating power — "held out until shadow-mode calibration proves it adds value" now points here, not at an unspecified future judgment.
Matured labels and the derived reads persist with the run's audit record and embed into the **Portfolio Analysis memory partition** as durable learnings ([§Continuity and isolation](#continuity-and-isolation)); whether the scorecard gets its own UI surface is deferred alongside Trade Opportunities' matching display decision.

## Storage and display

Each run persists its per-holding verdicts, the per-holding **thesis ledger** (carried forward to seed the next run's continuity check), and the portfolio roll-up, together with the **run audit record** that makes the run traceable and reviewable — **designed** to carry the holdings snapshot it ran against, sources and retrieval timestamps, distilled findings, computed metrics and derived reads, the input delta and per-value what-changed attribution, the price-target methodology, model and prompt/schema versions, degraded-input flags, each holding's **research-reuse decision** ([§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 3), and this run's **outcome-learning records** ([§Outcome learning](#outcome-learning-calibration)) — with the field set, which fields each verdict branch records, and the **as-built wired subset** all specified once in [storage.md §Local Analysis Suite Storage](storage.md#local-analysis-suite-storage).
(In the full design, source URLs, timestamps, and the distilled findings make a verdict's basis inspectable; full page snapshots are never stored, so a run is traceable rather than bit-for-bit reproducible.)
Retention keeps the last N runs (parallel to report retention — [storage.md](storage.md)); the retained runs are browsable from the shared-history sidebar, an older one opening read-only ([interface.md §Main Layout](interface.md#main-layout)).
The **Portfolio page** renders each holding's verdict.
Since `portfolio-v7` the priced card's body is the **paired two-arm grid**: the **engine baseline** column (sub-score tiles with the set-apart Setup read, the stand-in conviction / outlook / action, the engine targets, the methodology reveal) beside the **model view** column (the model's own tiles, its derived letter as a compact chip, its bands rendered exactly as authored with an `band inverted as authored` annotation where bear exceeds bull, conviction / outlook / action with quiet `≠ engine` tags on divergence).
The **portfolio action** is a full-width strip beneath the arms, and the **model retrospective** a prose block beside the financial analysis.
A pre-v7 run renders the legacy single intrinsic column; the extension is a recorded design-system deviation — the kit has no paired-comparison component, so comparison rides the system's adjacency + kicker idiom.
A **`role_risk_only`** verdict renders its own explicit card branch instead: the role read, exposure tilt, observable risk, expense drag, structural flag, and evidence gaps beside the action, plus the ledger's **condition-only** bear/base/bull monitor (no engine targets exist on that branch — [§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)), a designed state with **no empty priced-field placeholders** ([§Intrinsic verdict](#intrinsic-verdict)).
Every card is **anchored by the thesis ledger's current standing thesis** as its *why we hold this view*, rendered straight from the ledger the continuity check carries forward and rewrites ([§The position thesis ledger](#the-position-thesis-ledger)).
It is **not** a separately authored summary, so there is one continuity-validated source of truth and no drift between what the job carries forward and what the user reads — the held-position analog of each Trade Opportunity leading with its directional thesis.
The card carries financials and the what-changed line, plus the amber, actionable **attention flag** when the quick check has flagged the holding, retained until the next successful full pass over it clears and acknowledges the trigger ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
It carries a per-card **selection control** (with select-all / deselect-all) driving selective re-analysis ([§Triggering](#triggering)), and — after a selective run — an **analysis-vintage stamp on each carried card** (a current-vintage card is unstamped, reading as the run's own date), so mixed-vintage verdicts read as labeled vintages rather than a blend.
All of this renders alongside the portfolio roll-up, and the page shows not-rated and insufficient-evidence positions with their reason (see [interface.md](interface.md)).

**The current-holdings view.**
The page also carries the latest standalone **Pull holdings** snapshot ([§Triggering](#triggering)) as a compact, position-level view — symbol, quantity, the per-unit price the app **derives** as market value ÷ signed net quantity (Schwab reports no price field at all — [schwab-integration.md §What is pulled](schwab-integration.md#what-is-pulled); absent where net quantity is zero, which is the only case that leaves it null, and for option contracts it is therefore the per-contract value the source's market value implies, not a per-share quote), market value, cost basis, the percentage gain on cost (the card sort bar's same per-position-totals ratio, rendered as a directional value), and a pulled-at stamp.
Every column head sorts the rows **in place** — symbol alphabetically (opens ascending); quantity, price, market value, cost basis, and percentage gain numerically (open descending), a position missing a key's value (no reported price or cost basis) sorting last under that key in either direction, the card sort bar's convention — defaulting to the account's as-pulled order, with the last-used column remembered in webview `localStorage` like the card sort.
Being a real data grid, its heads carry **`aria-sort`** through keyboard-operable head buttons — the pattern the card-stack sort bar deliberately reserves for tables.
**Before any run exists it *is* the page body** (holdings viewable with no analysis — and no local-model configuration — at all, framed as *pulled, not yet analyzed* with Run analysis as the call to action); **once runs exist it appears only when the pull is fresher than the last run**, as a stamped section *above* the verdict cards — **never replacing them** — so fresh quantities and the run's verdicts read as two labeled vintages (*holdings as of…* / *analysis from…*) rather than a blend.
Position churn between the two vintages is tagged by **symbol presence only**: a pulled symbol absent from the run's snapshot reads *new · not in last analysis*, and a verdict card whose symbol has left the pull reads *no longer held* (the card itself stays, untouched — run output is never mutated or hidden by a pull).
Quantity-move classification is deliberately **not** re-derived here — that stays the run-time engine diff's job, with its short-position and reversal handling ([§Holdings change tracking](#holdings-change-tracking)) — and the *no-longer-held* tag is provisional until the next run formalizes the exit in the roll-up's closed-positions record.

**Holdings sorting.**
Above the holding-card stack a compact **sort bar** reorders the cards **in place** — no separate table view, so every card keeps its full intrinsic-verdict / portfolio-action / thesis-anchor content.
It renders only when more than one card exists — a single card has nothing to reorder.
It offers four deterministic, **position-level** keys, all engine-computed (never the model's grade or conviction — sorting by position size or P/L is orthogonal to the analysis read) from two **per-position account-currency totals** — the position's **market value** (Schwab-reported, so it already accounts for option contract multipliers, bond conventions, and cash balances) and its **cost basis** (the app-derived signed total, which does **not** apply the option contract / bond par multiplier — [schwab-integration.md §What is pulled](schwab-integration.md#what-is-pulled)) — rather than a naïve `quote × shares` that would misprice options, fixed income, and cash: **overall value** (market value), **dollar gain** (market value − cost basis, the same unrealized P/L the action call's evidence digest reads — [§Portfolio action](#portfolio-action)), **percentage gain** (that gain over cost basis), and **total cash invested** (cost basis).
A position Schwab reports **no cost basis** for (cash, typically) has an **undefined gain and cash-invested**, so it sorts **last** under the dollar-gain, percentage-gain, and cash-invested keys (the holdings analog of a debut Trade Opportunity's null since-flagged read), while still sorting normally by overall value.
A **negative netted basis** (a net-short book, where the short side's proceeds push the basis below zero — [schwab-integration.md §What is pulled](schwab-integration.md#what-is-pulled)) keeps its **dollar gain** — signed totals hold market value − cost basis equal to the book's aggregate unrealized P/L — but has **no honest percentage gain or cash-invested**, so both render **undefined** and sort last under those two keys; an **exactly-zero** netted basis is indistinguishable on the wire from an unreported basis, so it takes the no-basis treatment above (all three keys undefined); a zero, negative, or missing denominator never produces a divide-by-zero rank.
The default is **overall value, descending** (biggest positions first), tie-broken alphabetically by ticker; the last-used key is remembered as **ephemeral frontend state** in webview `localStorage` — the same store the Light/Dark appearance preference uses (pure presentation, no backend consumer), not the SQLite config store.
**Not-rated** positions (options / fixed income / cash) carry no grade but a real market value (and cost basis where Schwab reports one — cash typically has none), so they sort by **overall value** alongside graded holdings, kept visually tagged as not-rated — while **option and fixed-income rows withhold every cost-derived figure** (cost basis, average cost, dollar and percentage gain), the derived basis leaving the contract / par multiplier unapplied, so those render undefined and sort **last** under the dollar-gain, percentage-gain, and cash-invested keys exactly as an unreported basis does, until the `averagePrice` multiplier probe settles the convention; **exited** positions (closed since last run) live only in the roll-up ([§Holdings change tracking](#holdings-change-tracking)), never in the sortable stack.
The control is **display-only** — it reorders already-computed fields and touches no engine, schema, or workflow — and follows the project's frontend-craft requirements (keyboard-operable **toggle buttons** exposing the active key via `aria-pressed` plus a screen-reader-announced current-sort state (the active button's **accessible name carries the key *and* its sort direction** — e.g. "Sort by value, descending" — since the `▾`/`▴` glyph is decorative / `aria-hidden` and can't be relied on to announce direction) — *not* `aria-sort`, which is reserved for grid/table column headers such as Trade Opportunities' List view, not this card-stack toolbar; a stable sort; and a reduced-motion-respecting reflow).
The sort bar is a small **design-system extension**: it ports the `.ana-grid` sortable-header vocabulary (the neutral `▾` / `▴` glyph) to a non-table toolbar of tracked-caps triggers, since the holdings surface is cards rather than a data grid — no off-system dropdown or pill control ([interface.md](interface.md)).

## Failure posture

The execution gate requires the local model daemon and roster ([local-models.md](local-models.md)), **a connected Schwab account** ([schwab-integration.md](schwab-integration.md)) — holdings and the options signal both come from Schwab, so a missing or lapsed connection blocks the job (manual-import holdings do not satisfy the gate) — and the shared **FMP / FRED credential presence** ([portfolio-workflow.md §Step 1](portfolio-workflow.md#step-1-job-start-and-gate)).
Because holdings are independent, the job is designed to **checkpoint per holding** — each holding's completed stages persisting, so a cancellation or a single model failure resumes the unfinished holdings rather than restarting the (potentially hours-long) run, with recent research cached within the ~4-week freshness window (the same cache the graduated research-depth reuse reads — [§The per-holding pipeline](#the-per-holding-pipeline) Step 3); a selective re-analysis checkpoints identically.
**As-built only the between-holdings cancellation checkpoint exists** — mid-run per-holding persistence and the resume entry path below are **designed, not built** (queued in the Portfolio completion block), so today a failed or cancelled run persists no partial work and every run starts fresh.
**Resume is its own entry path, distinct from a new run** — the checkpoint contract is only coherent against one holdings snapshot.
A **resume** — offered from the failed / cancelled run's tracker state, only while its checkpoints exist and its pinned pull is younger than the drafted resume window ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)) — **reopens the interrupted run's ID and pins everything from it**: the original holdings pull and option-chain snapshot, the Step-5 shared context, and the model / prompt / schema versions, with **no new pull** — so completed and resumed holdings alike are computed against one coherent snapshot, and the finished run is honestly stamped with that pull's as-of time.
A **new run** — what the Run analysis control always starts — takes a fresh pull under a new run ID and **discards any interrupted run's checkpoints** (its partial verdicts never became a persisted run — the run-is-never-a-report analog); the per-holding **research cache** is designed to survive independently under its ~4-week window, so a discard would re-spend interpretation calls, not the web research (no cache exists while the research stage is stubbed).
Within a run, web research is fail-soft by design (thinner evidence, lower conviction — the stage is stubbed as-built), while a hard model or persistence failure fails the run, recorded like any other job ([scheduling.md](scheduling.md)).
The complete split, in one place — the failure matrix [scheduling.md](scheduling.md) delegates here.
**Hard, fails the run** — the Step-2 Schwab positions pull ([schwab-integration.md §Failure posture](schwab-integration.md#failure-posture)), a **run-level rate-anchor load failure**, a per-holding model-call failure inside the required 6c–6f analysis path (the action call included; as-built the failed run persists no partial work — the designed checkpoint/resume path above is what will make it resumable), and the run-persistence write.
Since `portfolio-v9` no post-loop model stage exists to fail: the retired construction era's **degraded rows** (a construction-failed run's per-holding work, persisted with no book) survive in retained stores — listed in the history with a **no book** tag, opening read-only, excluded from `latest_run` and every carry / diff / quick-check baseline via the persisted `constructed` marker, with the refusal surfaces still distinguishing a degraded-only store from an unreadable one.
The rate-anchor failure is the Step-5 FRED `DGS10` / `DGS2` retrieval still failing after the shared bounded retries ([data-sources.md §FRED](data-sources.md#fred-federal-reserve-economic-data)): the engine consumes the rates numerically in every target and hurdle, so the run fails **before any per-holding work is spent** rather than computing them off a stale or guessed print.
This is the suite's **canonical rate-anchor rule** — Trade Opportunities' failure posture points here, and the engine-only quick paths instead fail-soft to the cached print under the drafted max age ([§Starting parameters](#starting-parameters-calibratable)).
**Fail-soft, degrades** — the optional pre-loop held-name refresh lane, web research, and every enriching feed (FINRA, CBOE, the commodity context, CFTC positioning, option chains), each to its typed gap or degraded-input flag (of these only option chains are built — the lane, web research, and the designed feeds land with their slices).
**Per-holding, never run-level** — missing floor-bearing inputs exit that holding as `insufficient-evidence` at Step 6b ([§Evidence floor](#evidence-floor)).
And the engine-only **quick check** types each failed signal family **`unknown`** — never a silent clear — force-including the holding in a selective run; only the rate legs fail-soft to a cached print under the drafted max age, and a failed price refresh has no cache to fall to ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)).
A pre-loop refresh failure never clears a ledger condition, invalidates the cache, or force-includes on silence; it records `unscorable` and leaves the normal full-research max-age rule in charge.
The **quick check** is engine-only — no model call (it runs even configured-but-down, like Trade Opportunities' Quick Audit), no web research, no Schwab call — so its failure surface is the local store and its refresh retrievals ([§The quick check](#the-quick-check-engine-only)) — surfaced through the typed per-family sweep states, so a failed retrieval is visible and selective-run-safe rather than indistinguishable from a clean pass.
